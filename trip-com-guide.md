# Action Card: Trip.com Booking & Train Station Deadlocks

## Trigger
You booked a hotel or train on Trip.com, but at the station, the automatic gate rejects your passport. Or the hotel front desk refuses to check you in.

## Immediate Action
For train gates: Do NOT use the automatic turnstiles. Walk to the far left and look for the "Manual Channel" (人工通道). Hand your physical passport to the officer.

## Show This / Action
For hotel rejection: Do NOT argue. Open the Trip.com app and contact English customer service. Say: "This hotel is refusing to register my foreign passport. Please call them or move me to a Foreign Guest Friendly hotel for free."

## Safety / Don't Do
When booking hotels on Trip.com, ALWAYS tick the "Foreign Guest Friendly" filter. Do not book cheap domestic budget chains, as they often legally cannot host foreign passports. Do not buy train tickets from scalpers.

## Alternative
If your train is cancelled or delayed, go to the airline/train service desk and show your original booking. If the hotel refuses you, Trip.com will cover the cost of moving you to a compliant hotel.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou South Station, Shanghai Hongqiao Station.
- **Applies To:** All high-speed rail and Trip.com hotel bookings.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/trip-guide.html)
