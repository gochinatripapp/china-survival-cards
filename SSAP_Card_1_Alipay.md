1. TRIGGER (When This Happens)
You are at an airport, train station, hotel, or on a street. You need to book a ride, but you are unsure where the physical pickup point is, how to communicate with the driver, whether your luggage will fit, or how to avoid driver scams.

2. THE CHOICE (Big Picture Accounting)
Priority 1: Match Your Luggage to the Vehicle BEFORE Booking.

The Physical Reality: Standard DiDi Express (快车) trunks typically fit a maximum of 2 suitcases. Drivers are NOT obligated to help you lift luggage. If you force 3 large bags into an Express car, you will spend 10 minutes struggling on the street while the driver watches.

The Rational Choice: If you have 2+ large suitcases (24-inch+), do NOT book Express. Pre-book a Transfer on Trip.com (携程接送机). Official policy states: the driver holds a name sign at the arrival exit, provides free waiting time (often unlimited for delays), and assists with luggage. You skip the pin-dropping and luggage-confirmation stress entirely. An extra $5 buys you dignity.

If you insist on DiDi: Choose Premier (专车) for a larger car and a more formal driver. Send the luggage message AFTER the driver accepts, BEFORE they arrive (see Section 4D).

Priority 2: Find the Physical Pickup Point FIRST (The Pin Trap).

At Airports/Train Stations: The ride-hailing pickup zone (网约车候车区) is never the taxi rank. Follow signs for “网约车”. Open the DiDi app; it auto-locates the station’s designated pickup point (小绿点) and provides in-app walking directions. Do not book until you are physically at or near this zone. If you book at the wrong exit (e.g., East vs. West Square), the driver cannot reach you.

At Hotels/Streets: Zoom in and drag the pin to your exact side of the road or building entrance. If you are on the wrong side of a divided road, the driver will circle and cancel.

Priority 3: Pay Through the App, Never Cancel for Cash.
If a driver asks you to cancel the order and pay cash or scan a personal QR code, REFUSE. Once cancelled, all platform safety and dispute mechanisms are void.

3. IMMEDIATE ACTION (Step-by-Step)
Step 1: Destination Accuracy
Same-name stations: Search in English. Do not click the first result blindly. Check the Chinese name below the result. For train stations, ensure it says “北站” (North), “南站” (South), etc. Booking the wrong station can cost you double the fare and a missed train.

Save Favorites: Save your hotel as a Favorite to avoid re-typing and pin-dropping errors.

Step 2: Payment & Registration Setup
The Path: If direct foreign card linking fails (which is common due to bank risk control), use the Alipay/WeChat Mini-Program to hail the DiDi and pay through your Alipay balance.

SMS Trap: If you don't receive the verification SMS (often blocked by roaming carriers), try connecting to hotel Wi-Fi or using a local eSIM.

Step 3: Verify Before You Open the Door
The Only Rule: Match the License Plate. Check the last 3-4 characters on the app against the physical car. If they do not match, do NOT get in.

Do NOT match the photo. It is often dark, and the driver may be wearing a mask. The license plate is your only physical anchor.

The driver may ask for the last 4 digits of your phone number for verification.

Step 4: Communication (In-App Only)
Never answer the phone call. Drivers do not speak English. If you answer, you will waste time and cause confusion.

Use the In-App Chat. The standalone English DiDi app has built-in real-time translation: you type English, the driver sees Chinese.

If you cannot find the driver, send a chat message (see Section 4B).

Step 5: The “Cancel for Cash” Trap
If the driver asks you to cancel and pay cash: REFUSE.

Say or type: “No, we keep the app order.”

If they persist, do not get in. Cancel through the app (accept any small fee) and book again.

4. SHOW THIS (In-App Chat Templates)
A. For the DiDi Chat Box (copy and paste; app auto-translates):

师傅您好，我不会说中文。麻烦您通过滴滴软件发文字消息联系我，谢谢！
(Hello driver, I don’t speak Chinese. Please contact me via text in the DiDi app. Thank you.)

B. If you cannot find the car:

我在上车点这里，穿[颜色]衣服。麻烦您到了之后按一下双闪灯，谢谢！
(I am at the pickup point, wearing [color]. Please turn on your hazard lights when you arrive. Thank you.)

C. If the driver asks you to cancel and pay cash:

不用了，我坚持用平台订单。谢谢。
(No, I will keep the platform order. Thank you.)

D. For luggage issues (send AFTER driver accepts, BEFORE they arrive):

师傅您好，我有3个24寸的大行李箱。请问您的后备箱能放下吗？如果放不下，麻烦您告诉我，我取消订单换一辆大车。谢谢！
(Hello driver, I have 3 large 24-inch suitcases. Can your trunk fit them? If not, please let me know so I can cancel and book a larger car. Thank you!)

5. SAFETY / DON’T DO
NEVER book at an airport/train station before physically reaching the designated “网约车上车点”. The driver cannot pick you up from the taxi rank.

NEVER get into a car if the license plate does not match the app.

NEVER cancel the app order to pay cash. You lose all platform protection.

NEVER rely on phone calls. Use the in-app chat with auto-translation.

DO NOT assume Express cars will help with luggage. They won’t.

DO NOT hand your phone to a driver while they are driving.

DO NOT use “Red Packets” or personal transfers to test a foreign card.
