# SSAP Survival Action Card #1: Alipay Counter Rescue

**Version:** 2.0.0
**Last Verified:** November 12, 2026
**Protocol Standard:** SSAP (Survival Semantic Action Protocol) by GoChinaTrip.app

---

## Trigger (When This Happens)
You are paying a restaurant bill using an eligible foreign-issued bank card linked to Alipay. Your bill exceeds RMB 200, and either you want to understand the potential service fee, or your payment has failed.

---

## Immediate Action (What to Do First)
1. Stop attempting further payments. Check Alipay transaction records. Ask the cashier to check if the merchant received the payment.
2. If pending or unconfirmed, DO NOT initiate another payment to prevent double-charging.
3. If definitively unsuccessful, proceed to alternative methods. Safety before savings.

---

## Alternative / Backup (If the First Plan Fails)
- **Scenario A (Fee Avoidance):** Ask the merchant to process the bill as multiple separate transactions (each <= 200 RMB). Merchant may refuse. Do not insist.
- **Scenario B (Payment Decline):** Use physical bank card at POS terminal or offer cash.

---

## Show This (Full-Screen Chinese Cards for Staff)

**1. Asking to Split the Bill:**
> 不好意思，请问能不能麻烦您帮我分两笔扫？十分感谢！
> *(English: Excuse me, could you please split the bill into two payments? Thank you very much!)*

**2. If the Merchant Refuses to Split (Accept gracefully):**
> 好的，没关系！那请您直接扫我一笔全款吧。看我的付款码，能一次付款成功吗？谢谢！
> *(English: No problem at all! Then please just scan my full amount directly. Can you scan my payment code for one transaction? Thank you!)*

**3. Asking to Use a Foreign Card Before Ordering:**
> 你好，能刷外卡吗？
> *(English: Hello, can I use a foreign credit card here?)*

---

## Safety / Don't Do
- **NEVER** use personal transfers or "Red Packets" to test foreign cards, as this triggers risk-control freezes.
- **NEVER** screenshot your payment code; always present the live screen.
- **Do NOT** argue with the cashier if they refuse to split the bill. It is a favor, not a right.
- **Do NOT** pay the 3% fee if you can use a physical card or split the payment. But if all else fails, pay the fee to preserve dignity and time.

---

## Evidence & Field Test
- **Level 1 (Official):** The 200 RMB threshold is a platform fee boundary, not a hard transaction limit (Source: Alipay Help Center).
- **Level 2 (Documented Field Test):** The script and split-payment request were successfully used at physical restaurants in Guangzhou, November 2026. Merchants may refuse based on their internal bookkeeping rules.
