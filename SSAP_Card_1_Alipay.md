# SURVIVAL ACTION CARD 01: Alipay Counter Rescue

**Version:** 2.3.2
**Category:** Payments / Restaurant Checkout

### Mandatory Metadata
- **Evidence Type:** Official Policy + Human Field Observation
- **Applies To:** Foreign-issued Visa/Mastercard linked to Alipay in mainland China.
- **Last Verified:** November 2026
- **Known Limitations:** Merchant POS terminals and bank risk controls can vary. This card does not guarantee payment success. Bill splitting is not guaranteed to avoid fees.

---

## 1. TRIGGER (When This Happens)
You are checking out at a restaurant in mainland China using a foreign-issued card linked to Alipay. Your transaction may be subject to a 3% service fee (applied to the full transaction amount when exceeding 200 RMB), or your payment is declined.

---

## 2. THE CHOICE (Big Picture Accounting)
Do not fall into the trap of "having it all."
- **Priority: Smoothness:** Choose to pay the clearly displayed service fee in exchange for a potentially faster checkout and less negotiation. 
- **Priority: Savings:** Politely ask the manager if they can split the bill. If they agree, check the fee shown for *each* proposed payment. If they refuse, accept gracefully (waiters often lack the system permission).
- **Priority: Additional Fallback:** Ask if the merchant accepts a physical foreign card or RMB cash before relying on it.

---

## 3. IMMEDIATE ACTION (If the Payment Fails)
**STOP. NEVER retry an unclear transaction.** This is the most critical protection against double-charging.

1. Check the Alipay transaction record. 
2. Ask the merchant to verify if they have received the payment:
> 麻烦您帮我核对一下商家是否已经收到这笔款项，可以吗？谢谢！
> *(Could you check whether your business has received this payment? Thank you.)*
3. If the status remains pending or unclear, **DO NOT initiate another payment** until the first transaction's outcome has been established.
4. **The Endpoint:** Only if the transaction is confirmed failed AND the merchant confirms no payment was received, you may try another agreed payment method (e.g., physical card or cash).

---

## 4. SHOW THIS (Full-Screen Chinese Cards)

**A. Accepting the fee & paying:**
> 好的，没关系，我直接支付全款。谢谢！
> *(That's fine. I'll pay the full amount directly. Thank you!)*

**B. Politely asking to split (Conditional on Manager):**
> 不好意思，请问可以请经理帮忙分成两笔付款吗？如果不方便也没关系。
> *(Could I ask the manager to split this into two payments? No problem if it's inconvenient.)*

**C. Asking for physical card / cash:**
> 请问可以直接刷外国发行的 Visa 或 Mastercard 吗？或者用人民币现金？
> *(Can I pay directly with a foreign Visa/Mastercard, or RMB cash?)*

---

## 5. SAFETY / DON'T DO
- **NEVER** retry an unclear or pending transaction.
- **NEVER** use personal transfers or "Red Packets" to test an overseas card. These are unsupported payment scenarios and do not verify whether your card works for merchant purchases.
- Do not assume a merchant must split a bill for you.
