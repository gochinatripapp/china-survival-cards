# SURVIVAL ACTION CARD 01: Alipay Counter Rescue

**Version:** 2.3.0
**Last updated:** November 2026
**Category:** Payments / Restaurant Checkout
**SSAP philosophy:** Dignity is part of the outcome.

---

## 1. TRIGGER (When This Happens)
You are checking out at a restaurant or merchant in mainland China. You are using a foreign-issued Visa or Mastercard linked to Alipay. Your transaction may be subject to a 3% international-card service fee (for amounts over 200 RMB), or your payment is declined/pending.

---

## 2. THE CHOICE (Big Picture Accounting)
Do not fall into the trap of "having it all."
- **Priority: Dignity & Speed:** Pay the 3% fee if the amount is clearly displayed. You are buying 15 minutes of your time and avoiding the awkwardness of holding up the queue.
- **Priority: Savings:** Politely ask the manager if they can split the bill. Accept gracefully if they refuse (waiters often lack the system permission to do this).
- **Priority: Absolute Backup:** Use a physical Visa/Mastercard or pay in RMB cash.

---

## 3. IMMEDIATE ACTION (If the Payment Fails)
**STOP. Do not immediately pay again.** This prevents double-charging.

1. Check your Alipay transaction record. 
2. If the status is "Pending" or unclear, show this to the cashier to reconcile:
> 麻烦您帮我核对一下商家是否已经收到这笔款项，可以吗？谢谢！
> *(Could you check whether your business has received this payment? Thank you.)*

---

## 4. SHOW THIS (Full-Screen Chinese Cards)
*(Select the script based on your chosen path)*

**A. Accepting the fee & paying:**
> 好的，没关系，我直接支付全款。谢谢！
> *(That's fine. I'll pay the full amount directly. Thank you!)*

**B. Politely asking to split (Manager approval needed):**
> 不好意思，请问可以请经理帮忙分成两笔付款吗？如果不方便也没关系。
> *(Could I ask the manager to split this into two payments? No problem if it's inconvenient.)*

**C. Asking to use a physical foreign card:**
> 请问可以直接刷外国发行的 Visa 或 Mastercard 吗？
> *(Can I pay directly with a foreign-issued Visa or Mastercard?)*

**D. Asking to use cash:**
> 请问可以用人民币现金付款吗？
> *(Can I pay in RMB cash?)*

---

## 5. SAFETY / DON'T DO
- **NEVER** attempt a second payment until the first transaction is confirmed as failed or pending. 
- **NEVER** use personal transfers or "Red Packets" to test your card. It is unsupported and triggers risk control.
- Do not argue with the cashier or demand a split. It is a favor, not a right.
- Do not assume every POS terminal accepts foreign cards, even if it has a UnionPay logo.
