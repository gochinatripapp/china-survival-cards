# SURVIVAL ACTION CARD 01: Alipay Counter Rescue

**Version:** 2.2.0
**Last updated:** November 2026
**Category:** Payments / Restaurant Checkout
**SSAP philosophy:** Dignity is part of the outcome.

---

> ## DIGNITY FIRST
> **Do not fight the physical world to save 15 RMB.**
> 
> A busy restaurant is not the place to negotiate unusual payment arrangements or force a waiter to seek manager approval for a split bill. 
> 
> If a legitimate 3% cross-border fee is clearly disclosed and the payment works, paying it is the most comfortable and dignified choice. 
> 
> Prepare before ordering. Keep a physical bank card and some RMB cash as backup.

---

## 1. TRIGGER (When This Happens)
You are checking out at a restaurant in mainland China. You are using a foreign card bound to Alipay. Your transaction is declined, pending, or over 200 RMB (subject to a 3% fee). 

---

## 2. PREVENTION FIRST (Before You Order)
**Ask before you sit down.** Show the cashier this script:
> 你好，请问这里可以使用绑定在支付宝上的外国 Visa 或 Mastercard 付款吗？
> *(Hello. Can I pay here using a foreign Visa or Mastercard linked to Alipay?)*

Keep your physical Visa/Mastercard and 100-200 RMB cash ready as a backup.

---

## 3. IMMEDIATE ACTION (If the Payment Fails)
**STOP. Do not immediately pay again.** This prevents double-charging.

1. Check your Alipay transaction record.
2. If the status is "Pending" or unclear, show this to the cashier to reconcile:
> 麻烦您帮我核对一下商家是否已经收到这笔款项，可以吗？谢谢！
> *(Could you check whether your business has received this payment? Thank you.)*

---

## 4. ALTERNATIVE / BACKUP (Graceful Solutions)
- **Option A: Pay the fee.** If the fee is clear and the payment works, just pay it. Preserve your time and dignity.
- **Option B: Physical Card.** Ask: `请问可以直接刷外国发行的 Visa 或 Mastercard 吗？` *(Can I pay directly with a foreign card?)*. If yes, use the physical card; the merchant usually absorbs the fee.
- **Option C: Cash.** Ask: `请问可以用人民币现金付款吗？` *(Can I pay in RMB cash?)*

**The "Split Bill" Exception:**
Do not assume the cashier can split the bill. If you want to ask, you MUST ask for the manager and accept refusal gracefully:
> 不好意思，请问可以请经理帮忙分成两笔付款吗？如果不方便也没关系。
> *(Could I ask the manager to split this into two payments? No problem if it's inconvenient.)*

If they say no, smile and say:
> 好的，没关系，我直接支付全款。谢谢！
> *(That's fine. I'll pay the full amount directly. Thank you!)*

---

## 5. SAFETY / DON'T DO
- **NEVER** attempt a second payment until the first transaction is confirmed as failed. 
- **NEVER** use personal transfers or "Red Packets" to test your card. It will not work and will trigger risk control.
- Do not argue with the cashier or insist on splitting the bill. It is a favor, not a right.
- Do not screenshot your payment QR code; open the live app.

---

## 6. EVIDENCE
- **Level 1 (Official):** The 200 RMB fee threshold applies to the transaction amount, not just the excess.
- **Level 2 (Field Observation):** Based on 10 years of restaurant ownership in Guangzhou. POS machines are cashier-controlled. Splitting a bill requires manager approval and is often refused during peak hours.
