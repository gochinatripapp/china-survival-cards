# Action Card: Foreign Guest Friendly Hotels & Rejections

## Trigger
You booked a hotel online. You arrive at the front desk, but the receptionist hands your passport back and refuses to check you in because they lack the license to host foreigners.

## Immediate Action
Do NOT argue with the front desk staff. It is a system licensing issue, not their personal choice.

## Show This / Action
Open the Trip.com App immediately and call English customer service. Read this exact script:
"This hotel is refusing to register my foreign passport. I have a confirmed booking. Please call them right now or move me to a hotel that accepts foreigners for free."

## Safety / Don't Do
Booking.com and Agoda do NOT filter for hotel foreigner licenses. You can be rejected at midnight. You MUST book on Trip.com and tick the "Foreign Guest Friendly" filter.

## Alternative
If they ask for a large deposit (e.g., 3,000 RMB), do NOT use Alipay (you will pay a 3% cross-border fee). Hand them your physical Visa/Mastercard for a pre-authorization hold. The hotel absorbs the fee.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** November 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou & Shanghai hotel check-ins.
- **Applies To:** All cities in mainland China.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/hotel-guide.html)
