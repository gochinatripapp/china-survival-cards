# Action Card: Xi'an Terracotta Warriors & Muslim Quarter Traps

## Trigger
You arrive at the train station and touts offer a "$50 day tour" to the Terracotta Warriors, or you go to the Muslim Quarter main street and get ripped off by tourist traps.

## Immediate Action
For Terracotta: Ignore all touts. Walk to the official bus stop and take the green Bus 306 (Line 5). Buy the official audio guide when you arrive.
For Food: Do NOT eat on the main Beiyuanmen street. Walk 10 minutes deeper to Sajinqiao or Dapiyuan for authentic, cheap local food.

## Show This / Action
If a taxi driver tries to take you to a "special" restaurant: "不用了，谢谢，我已经吃过了。" (No thanks, I have already eaten.) Do not let them drop you at a commission-based tourist trap.

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT buy "VIP Fast Passes" or fake jade. If you ride a bike on the City Wall, test the brakes first. The full 13.7km loop is bumpy; a half-loop from South Gate to West Gate is better.

## Alternative
If the Terracotta Warriors are sold out (you must book 3-7 days ahead with your passport), visit the Shaanxi History Museum instead (also requires advance booking).
