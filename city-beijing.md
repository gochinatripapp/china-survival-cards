# Action Card: Beijing Forbidden City & Hutong DiDi Deadlock

## Trigger
You arrive at the Forbidden City without a ticket, or you call a DiDi in a narrow Hutong and the driver cancels because he can't find you.

## Immediate Action
For the Forbidden City: There are NO ticket windows. You must book 7 days in advance via the WeChat Mini-Program with your passport. Do not buy from scalpers.
For DiDi: Hang up the phone. Use the DiDi in-app chat to say: "I am at the pin. The street is narrow. Can you wait at the main road? I will walk out."

## Show This / Action
If lost in a Hutong, show a local: "你好，我迷路了，能帮我指一下去大路的方向吗？" (Hello, I am lost, can you point me to the main road?).

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT buy street food on the main Wangfujing pedestrian street. For authentic food, go to Siji Minfu for Peking Duck, but use Dianping to check the queue first. Do not give the DiDi driver your 4-digit code until you are seated inside.

## Alternative
If you can't get Forbidden City tickets, go to the East Gate (Donghuamen) and ask staff if there are any same-day cancellations released. If DiDi fails, walk to a main road for a physical taxi.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Dongcheng & Xicheng districts.
- **Applies To:** Beijing city center.
