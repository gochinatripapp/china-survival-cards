SURVIVAL ACTION CARD 02: DiDi Communication, Pickup & Luggage Deadlock
Version: 2.3.4
Category: Transportation / Driver Communication

Mandatory Metadata
Evidence Type: Official Policy + Human Field Observation

Applies To: DiDi users with heavy luggage in mainland China.

Last Verified: November 2026

Known Limitations: Vehicle availability varies. A 7-seater does not automatically guarantee trunk space with all seats occupied. Pickup zones (especially at airports/stations) are strictly segregated. Do not assume the driver will help load or unload luggage. Confirm assistance with the provider in advance. App features (like translation) vary by app version and require network connectivity.

1. TRIGGER (When This Happens)
You arrive at an airport or train station and cannot find the physical DiDi pickup zone.

Your DiDi driver calls, but you cannot understand or speak Mandarin.

You cannot identify the vehicle or agree on the pickup location.

You have heavy luggage (e.g., 3 large suitcases) and are unsure if the car can accommodate them.

The driver asks for the last four digits of your phone number before departure.

2. PREVENTION FIRST — Physical Pickup & Capacity Check
Do not let a digital booking create a physical deadlock at the curb.

A. The Physical Pickup Zone (Location Deadlock):

In Chinese transport hubs, transport modes are physically separated. DiDi drivers are restricted from picking you up at the taxi rank or departure curb.

The Rule: Identify the designated "网约车" (Ride-Hailing) area. If the app requires you to select a pickup point (like a specific terminal or green dot), follow the in-app instructions and navigate to that exact physical meeting point. Do not assume the nearest exit is an authorized pickup zone.

The Consequence: If you are not at the agreed pickup point, the driver may be unable to reach you. Waiting time is limited by the booking rules, and the driver may cancel. A location mismatch can cause a failed pickup, wasted time, and possible charges.

B. The Capacity Check (Luggage Deadlock):

Priority: Smoothness: If you have three large 24-inch suitcases and 2-3 passengers, do not blindly book a standard Express. Consider a larger vehicle (e.g., 7-seater) or a pre-booked private transfer, but only after confirming the actual passenger capacity, luggage allowance, and vehicle configuration with the provider.

Priority: Savings: If you only have backpacks, a standard DiDi Express may be sufficient, depending on passenger numbers.

The Rule: A higher price or a 7-seat category does not automatically guarantee luggage space.

3. IMMEDIATE ACTION — The Driver Calls & Arrival
The Communication Boundary: If the driver calls, do not guess at instructions. Where available, use DiDi's in-app typed messaging to explain your exact pickup location. If the driver is already waiting and the situation is urgent, ask nearby staff for help. Do not assume translation is guaranteed in every app version. Never hang up blindly if they are physically at your location.

Verify the Vehicle: Match the license plate against your active booking before boarding.

The Verification Boundary: Follow the verification instructions displayed in your active booking. If the driver requests the last four digits of your registered phone number, check that it matches the booking's procedure before providing them. Never disclose an SMS login code, payment password, or other account authentication codes.

(Hello, we have three large 24-inch suitcases. Please open the trunk so we can confirm they all fit. Thank you!)

D. If the luggage does not fit — Resolve the Capacity Deadlock:

Do not force luggage into an unsafe or unsuitable space.

Explain the capacity problem to the driver.

Check the booking's cancellation and modification options before taking action.

Compare the applicable fee, time pressure, and available alternatives.

If the vehicle is unsuitable, arrange an appropriate alternative through a confirmed booking or provider.

Keep the original booking status and any payment or cancellation confirmation. Do not assume that a cancellation has completed until the app confirms it.

You may decide that paying a cancellation fee is worthwhile to save time, but the fee and alternatives should be clear before you decide. Do not assume that the charge is small, avoidable, or refundable.

5. SAFETY / DON'T DO
NEVER book a ride before identifying the physical "网约车" zone at airports/stations, or without matching the app's selected pickup point to your exact location.

NEVER board a vehicle if the license plate does not match the app.

NEVER disclose an SMS login code or payment password. The last four digits of your registered phone number may be used for ride verification, but are not an account password.

NEVER force oversized luggage into the passenger cabin.

NEVER blindly cancel or pay a fee without checking the app's terms.
