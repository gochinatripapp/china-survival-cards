# SURVIVAL ACTION CARD 02: DiDi Communication & Luggage Deadlock

**Version:** 2.3.2
**Category:** Transportation / Driver Communication

### Mandatory Metadata
- **Evidence Type:** Official Policy + Human Field Observation
- **Applies To:** DiDi users with heavy luggage in mainland China.
- **Last Verified:** November 2026
- **Known Limitations:** Vehicle availability varies. A 7-seater does not automatically guarantee trunk space with all seats occupied. Do not assume the driver will help load or unload luggage. Confirm assistance with the provider in advance.

---

## 1. TRIGGER (When This Happens)
- Your DiDi driver calls, but you cannot understand or speak Mandarin.
- You cannot identify the vehicle or agree on the pickup location.
- You have heavy luggage (e.g., 3 large suitcases) and are unsure if the car can accommodate them.
- The driver asks for the last four digits of your phone number before departure.

---

## 2. PREVENTION FIRST — Match Capacity Before Booking
Do not let a digital booking create a physical deadlock at the curb. 

**The "Three-Suitcase" Reality:**
- **Priority: Smoothness:** If you have three large suitcases and 2-3 passengers, do not blindly book a standard Express. Consider a larger vehicle (e.g., 7-seater) or a pre-booked private transfer, but **only after confirming the actual passenger capacity, luggage allowance, and vehicle configuration with the provider**.
- **Priority: Savings:** If you only have backpacks, a standard DiDi Express may be sufficient, depending on passenger numbers and the actual vehicle.
- **The Rule:** A higher price or a 7-seat category does not automatically guarantee luggage space.

---

## 3. IMMEDIATE ACTION — The Driver Calls
1. **The Communication Boundary:** If you cannot communicate effectively in Mandarin, do not guess or rely on a voice call. Use DiDi's in-app translated messaging to clarify the pickup. If the pickup is urgent or unclear, ask nearby hotel or station staff for help.
2. **Verify the Vehicle:** Match the **license plate** against your active booking before boarding.
3. **The Last-4-Digit Verification:** Before departure, follow the verification method displayed in your active DiDi booking.

---

## 4. SHOW THIS — Full-Screen Chinese Scripts

**A. Use In-App Chat:**
> 师傅您好，我不会说中文。麻烦您通过滴滴软件发文字消息联系我，谢谢！
> *(Hello, I can't speak Chinese. Please contact me via text in the DiDi app. Thank you!)*

**B. The Last-4-Digits Card (Fill in your own digits before showing):**
> **“我的注册手机号后四位是：____”**
> *(The last four digits of my registered phone number are: ____)*

**C. Luggage confirmation:**
> 师傅您好，我们有三个24寸的大行李箱。麻烦您打开后备箱，我们想先确认能不能全部放下。谢谢！
> *(Hello, we have three large 24-inch suitcases. Please open the trunk so we can confirm they all fit. Thank you!)*

**D. If the luggage does not fit — Exit the deadlock (Big Picture Accounting):**
1. Do not force the luggage into the vehicle. Safety first.
2. If the vehicle cannot accommodate the luggage, cancel the unsuitable booking through DiDi immediately.
3. Check the cancellation terms and accept the applicable small charge rather than spending valuable time disputing it when your travel deadline is at risk. **Time is the most expensive currency.**
4. Immediately arrange a more suitable vehicle or private transfer.

**Show This:**
> 不好意思，我们的行李太多，这辆车放不下。我们取消订单，重新叫一辆更大的车。给您添麻烦了，谢谢！
> *(Sorry, we have too much luggage and it won't fit. We'll cancel and book a larger car. Sorry for the inconvenience, and thank you!)*

---

## 5. SAFETY / DON'T DO
- **NEVER** board a vehicle if the license plate does not match the app.
- **NEVER** disclose an SMS login code or payment password. The last four digits of your registered phone number may be used for ride verification, but are not an account password.
- **NEVER** force oversized luggage into the passenger cabin.
