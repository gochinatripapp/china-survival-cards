# Action Card: Chengdu Panda Base Tickets & Hotpot Deadlock

## Trigger
You arrive at the Panda Base hoping to buy a ticket on the spot (but it's 100% real-name online), or you sit down for hotpot and the menu is entirely in Chinese.

## Immediate Action
For Pandas: You MUST book 14 days in advance via Trip.com or WeChat with your passport. Arrive at 7:30 AM. After 9:00 AM, the pandas go to sleep.
For Hotpot: Do NOT try to explain in English. Show the waiter a full-screen Chinese card to order a split pot.

## Show This / Action
Show this for hotpot: "老板，我们要微辣鸳鸯锅，清汤那边不要放辣椒，谢谢！" (Boss, we want a mild split pot, no chili on the clear soup side, thanks!)
If the food is too spicy, drink ice soy milk or sweet iced jelly (冰粉). Do NOT drink water.

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT buy panda tickets from scalpers. Do not trust a DiDi driver if he calls and says he can't enter the pedestrian zone (like Taikoo Li); tell him to wait at the main road.

## Alternative
If you can't get Panda Base tickets, go to Dujiangyan Panda Valley. If the hotpot is still too spicy, order a bowl of plain rice and dip the meat in sesame oil only.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Jinjiang & Wuhou districts.
- **Applies To:** Chengdu city center.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/city-chengdu.html)
