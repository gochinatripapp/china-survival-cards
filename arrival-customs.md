# Action Card: NIA 12367 Digital Arrival Card

## Trigger
You are flying to China. You heard there is a digital arrival card, but you are not sure when to fill it out or what happens if you don't.

## Immediate Action
Fill out the NIA 12367 form online within 72 hours before your flight. Do this at home on your own Wi-Fi, NOT at the airport.

## Show This / Action
Access the form via the official NIA website or the Alipay/WeChat Mini-Program. **Screenshot the generated QR code immediately after submitting.**

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT rely on airport Wi-Fi to load the form. Airport Wi-Fi often requires a Chinese phone number for SMS verification, which you won't have. Paper forms are a backup, but queues are long.

## Alternative
If you didn't fill it out online, look for a staff member in a blue vest near the immigration lanes and say "Paper card?". They will hand you the physical form.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Desktop review against official NIA portal.
- **Applies To:** All foreign passport holders entering mainland China.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/arrival-customs.html)
