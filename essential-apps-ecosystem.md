# Action Card: The 6 Essential Apps & 2 Mini-Programs for China

## Trigger
You are preparing for a trip to China. You heard that Google, WhatsApp, and Uber do not work. You need to know exactly what digital tools to install before boarding the plane.

## Immediate Action
Do NOT wait until you land. Download these 6 apps and register them on your home Wi-Fi.

## The 6 Essential Apps:
1. **Alipay (支付宝)**: Your primary wallet. Bind your Visa/Mastercard here first. It is more stable than WeChat for foreign cards.
2. **WeChat (微信)**: The operating system of China. Required for messaging, mini-programs, and some payments.
3. **Trip.com**: The only OTA with a "Foreign Guest Friendly" hotel filter. Book trains and airport transfers here.
4. **Amap (高德地图)**: The Chinese version of Google Maps. Google Maps is completely blind in China. Amap has an English interface.
5. **DiDi (滴滴出行)**: The Chinese Uber. You must download the mainland China version, not the international rider app.
6. **Doubao (豆包)**: An AI translation app built by ByteDance (TikTok's parent company). It works flawlessly without a VPN.

## The 2 Essential Mini-Programs (Inside WeChat/Alipay):
1. **NIA 12367 (国家移民管理局)**: For filling out the digital arrival card before your flight. Screenshot the QR code.
2. **Zhangjiajie Yi Ji You (张家界一机游)**: If visiting Zhangjiajie, this is the ONLY way to book real-name tickets.

## Show This / Action
If you need to scan a table QR code to order food, use WeChat or Alipay. If a restaurant doesn't have an English menu, use Doubao's camera translation.

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT download "AlipayHK" (Hong Kong version) or "DiDi Rider" (International version). They do not work properly in mainland China. Do NOT buy a local Chinese SIM card at the airport; buy an international eSIM before you fly to bypass the firewall.

## Alternative
If you forget to download an app, use the WeChat or Alipay Mini-Program versions. DiDi, Meituan (food), and Dianping (reviews) all have mini-programs.
