# Action Card: China Airport Arrival & Pickup Points

## Trigger
You just landed at a major Chinese airport (SZX, PEK, PVG, CAN). You need Wi-Fi, a taxi, or your pre-booked driver, but you cannot speak Chinese.

## Immediate Action
Do NOT rely on airport Wi-Fi to call a taxi. Airport Wi-Fi requires a Chinese phone number to log in. Ensure your eSIM is active before you land.

## Show This / Action
To find your DiDi or official taxi:
- **Shenzhen (SZX)**: Taxi is GTC 2nd Floor, East Platform, Door 13. DiDi is GTC Level 1.
- **Shanghai Pudong (PVG)**: Taxi is 1st Floor, Gate 26. DiDi is P2 Parking Garage, B2.
- **Beijing Daxing (PKX)**: DiDi is Gate 14 on the 2nd Floor (Former P2 Garage).
- **Guangzhou (CAN)**: Taxi is GTC Center, Gate 50/53. DiDi is P8 Garage 2F/3F.

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT follow unmarked taxi touts who approach you inside the terminal. Ignore them and follow the official signs to the taxi queue or DiDi zone.

## Alternative
If you are completely lost or your phone is dead, look for the "International Service Center" (境外来宾综合服务中心) in the arrivals hall for free Wi-Fi and English assistance.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** SZX, PVG, PKX, CAN airports.
- **Applies To:** Major international airport terminals.
