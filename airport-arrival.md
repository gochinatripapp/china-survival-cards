# ✈️ Action Card: China Airport Arrival & Pickup Points

## Mandatory Metadata
*   **Evidence Type:** Field Tested & Official Airport Directory
*   **Applies To:** Foreign travelers landing at major Chinese international airports.
*   **Last Verified:** 2026-11-27
*   **Known Limitations:** Airport layouts and specific pickup zones (especially DiDi zones) may change due to construction or local traffic control. Always verify on-site signage. Airport Wi-Fi requires a Chinese phone number.

---

## 1. TRIGGER
You just landed at a major Chinese airport (SZX, PEK, PKX, PVG, CAN). You need Wi-Fi, a taxi, or your pre-booked driver, but you cannot speak Chinese and the terminal is massive.

## 2. IMMEDIATE ACTION
*   **Wi-Fi Trap:** Do NOT rely on airport Wi-Fi to call a car. Airport Wi-Fi requires a Chinese phone number to log in via SMS. **Ensure your international eSIM is active before you land.**
*   **Ignore Touts:** Do NOT follow unmarked taxi touts who approach you inside the terminal. Ignore them and follow the official overhead signs to the taxi queue or the designated ride-hailing app zone (网约车).

## 3. ALTERNATIVE / BACKUP
*   **If your phone is dead:** Look for the "International Service Center" (境外来宾综合服务中心) in the arrivals hall for free Wi-Fi and English-speaking assistance.
*   **If you cannot find your DiDi:** Do not wander aimlessly. Walk back inside to the arrival hall, find a coffee shop or convenience store, connect to Wi-Fi, and use the in-app chat.
*   **The Ultimate Backup:** Walk to the official taxi queue (usually clearly marked with an icon of a car). Official cabs are metered and safe.

## 4. SHOW THIS (Action / Scripts)

**To find your Official Taxi or DiDi zone (Show this to airport staff):**
> 你好，请问出租车候车点/网约车上车点怎么走？
> (Hello, where is the taxi queue / DiDi pickup zone?)

**Verified Pickup Points (As of Nov 2026):**
*   **Shenzhen (SZX):** Official Taxi: GTC 2nd Floor, East Platform, Door 13. DiDi: GTC Level 1.
*   **Shanghai Pudong (PVG):** Official Taxi: T1/T2 1st Floor, exit through Gate 26. DiDi: P2 Parking Garage, B2 Level.
*   **Beijing Daxing (PKX):** DiDi: Gate 14 on the 2nd Floor, cross corridor to the new Ride-hailing Zone (Former P2 Garage).
*   **Guangzhou (CAN):** Official Taxi: GTC Transportation Center, outside Gate 50/53. DiDi: P8 Parking Garage 2F/3F.

## 5. SAFETY / DON'T DO
*   **DO NOT** get into an unmarked car if a stranger approaches you asking "Taxi? Taxi?".
*   **DO NOT** accept an offline cash price for an airport transfer. Use the meter or the app.
*   **DO NOT** leave your luggage unattended at the curb while waiting for a ride.
