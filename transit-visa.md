# Action Card: 240-Hour Transit Visa-Free Policy

## Trigger
You have a long layover in China and want to leave the airport, but you are unsure if you need a visa.

## Immediate Action
Check if you qualify for the 240-hour (10-day) transit visa-free policy. It applies to citizens of 54 countries (including US, UK, Australia, Canada).

## Show This / Action
You must have a confirmed onward ticket to a THIRD country (e.g., London -> Shanghai -> Tokyo). You cannot fly back to the country you came from. Follow the signs to the "Transit Without Visa" (TWOV) counter to get your temporary entry permit.

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT leave the airport without getting the temporary entry permit stamped at the TWOV counter. If your luggage is checked through to the final destination, leave it; just carry your carry-on.

## Alternative
If you get stuck in the city due to traffic and might miss your flight, contact your airline immediately. Show this: "你好，我是转机乘客，我现在在市区遇到了严重堵车，可能赶不上下一趟航班了，请问能帮我想办法吗？" (Hello, I am a transit passenger. I am stuck in traffic and might miss my flight. Can you help me?)

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou Baiyun (CAN), Shanghai Pudong (PVG).
- **Applies To:** 54 eligible countries.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/airport-transit-tours.html)
