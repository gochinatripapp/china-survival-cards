# SURVIVAL ACTION CARD 03: Zhangjiajie Ticket & Entry Verification

**Version:** 2.3.0
**Last updated:** November 2026
**Category:** Attractions / Ticket Booking
**Core Philosophy:** Official Rules First. 拒绝黄牛，保护行程。

---

## 1. TRIGGER (When This Happens)
You arrive at a Zhangjiajie National Forest Park entrance with an online booking but no physical ticket. Or, you discover you have not made a reservation. Touts nearby offer "internal tickets" or claim the park is sold out.

---

## 2. THE CHOICE (Big Picture Accounting)
Do not trade a potential loss of your entire trip for a few dollars.
- **Priority: Safety & Guaranteed Entry:** Always book official real-name tickets 2-7 days in advance via the official WeChat Mini-Program "张家界旅游小助手" (Zhangjiajie Travel Assistant). The system is strict, but it guarantees your entry.
- **Priority: Absolute Backup:** If official tickets are sold out, do not buy from scalpers. Change your itinerary date or visit another attraction. A fake ticket means a total loss, a fine, and a ruined day.

---

## 3. IMMEDIATE ACTION
1. **Ignore Touts:** Walk away from anyone offering unofficial tickets, guaranteed entry, or special access.
2. **Keep Your Passport:** A photograph is not a substitute for the original identity document. Entry requires a physical face-scan + passport match.
3. **Find the Manual Channel:** Foreign passports cannot pass the automatic turnstiles. Ask a uniformed staff member for the "人工核验" (manual verification window).
4. **Re-entry:** If you plan to leave and re-enter the park, you must complete a separate re-entry reservation before leaving.

---

## 4. SHOW THIS (Full-Screen Chinese Cards)

**A. Passport Verification:**
> 您好，我已经使用外国护照实名预约并购买了门票。请问持外国护照的游客应该在哪里进行人工证件核验和入园？这是我的护照和订单确认信息。麻烦您帮我确认一下，谢谢！
> *(Hello. I have made a real-name reservation and purchased my ticket using my foreign passport. Where should foreign passport holders go for manual document verification and park entry? Here are my passport and booking confirmation. Could you please help me check them? Thank you!)*

**B. No Reservation:**
> 您好，我还没有预约门票。请问今天还有余票吗？外国护照可以在哪里实名预约购票？
> *(Hello. I haven't booked a ticket yet. Are there any tickets available today? Where can foreign passport holders make a real-name reservation and purchase tickets?)*

---

## 5. SAFETY / DON'T DO
- **NEVER** buy tickets from scalpers or unofficial resellers.
- **NEVER** assume a booking for one entrance is valid at another entrance.
- **NEVER** buy a second ticket before checking whether your existing booking is valid.
- Do not argue with gate staff. Ask them to direct you to the ticket supervisor if needed.
