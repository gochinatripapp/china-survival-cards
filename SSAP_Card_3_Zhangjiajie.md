# SURVIVAL ACTION CARD 03: Zhangjiajie Ticket & Entry Verification

**Version:** 2.3.2
**Category:** Attractions / Ticket Booking

### Mandatory Metadata
- **Evidence Type:** Official Policy + Human Field Observation
- **Applies To:** Foreign passport holders visiting Zhangjiajie National Forest Park.
- **Last Verified:** November 2026
- **Known Limitations:** A booking confirmation is not proof of admission. Passport verification and face-scanning are required at the gate. Park entry policies may change.

---

## 1. TRIGGER (When This Happens)
You arrive at a Zhangjiajie National Forest Park entrance with an online booking. Touts nearby offer "internal tickets" or claim the park is sold out.

---

## 2. THE CHOICE (Big Picture Accounting)
Do not trade a potential loss of your entire trip for a few dollars.
- **Priority: Guaranteed Entry:** Always book official real-name tickets 2-7 days in advance via the official WeChat Mini-Program "张家界旅游小助手" (Zhangjiajie Travel Assistant). 
- **Priority: Absolute Backup:** If official tickets are sold out, do not buy from scalpers. Change your itinerary date or visit another attraction. A fake ticket means a total loss and a ruined day.

---

## 3. IMMEDIATE ACTION
1. **Ignore Touts:** Walk away from anyone offering unofficial tickets.
2. **Keep Your Passport:** A photograph is not a substitute. Entry requires a physical face-scan + passport match.
3. **Find the Manual Channel:** Foreign passports cannot pass automatic turnstiles. Ask staff for the "人工核验" (manual verification window).
4. **Re-entry:** You must complete a separate re-entry reservation before leaving the park.

---

## 4. SHOW THIS (Full-Screen Chinese Cards)

**A. Passport Verification:**
> 您好，我已经使用外国护照实名预约并购买了门票。请问持外国护照的游客应该在哪里进行人工证件核验和入园？这是我的护照和订单确认信息。麻烦您帮我确认一下，谢谢！
> *(Hello. I have made a real-name reservation using my foreign passport. Where should foreign passport holders go for manual document verification and park entry? Here are my passport and booking confirmation. Thank you!)*

**B. No Reservation:**
> 您好，我还没有预约门票。请问今天还有余票吗？外国护照可以在哪里实名预约购票？
> *(Hello. I haven't booked a ticket yet. Are there any tickets available today? Where can foreign passport holders make a real-name reservation?)*

---

## 5. SAFETY / DON'T DO
- **NEVER** buy tickets from scalpers or unofficial resellers.
- **NEVER** assume a booking for one entrance is valid at another entrance.
- **NEVER** buy a second ticket before checking whether your existing booking is valid.
