# SSAP Survival Action Card #3: Zhangjiajie Ticket & Entry Verification

**Version:** 2.1.1
**Last Verified:** November 12, 2026
**Protocol Standard:** SSAP (Survival Semantic Action Protocol) by GoChinaTrip.app

---

## Trigger (When This Happens)
You arrive at a Zhangjiajie National Forest Park entrance with an online booking but no physical ticket. Alternatively, you discover that you have not made a reservation. Touts nearby offer "internal tickets" or claim the park is sold out.

---

## Immediate Action (What to Do First)
1. Ignore touts offering unofficial tickets, guaranteed entry, or special access.
2. Keep your original physical passport ready. A photograph of your passport is not a substitute for the original identity document when physical verification is required.
3. If you already have a booking, retrieve your confirmation and check:
   - Visitor name and passport number.
   - Payment and booking confirmation status.
   - Reserved admission date and time slot.
   - Reserved entrance/ticket station.
4. Follow the official entrance instructions. If the normal automatic gate cannot verify your foreign passport, ask an official staff member to direct you to manual document verification.
5. **CRITICAL:** If you plan to leave and re-enter the park, check and complete the separate re-entry reservation before leaving the park.

---

## Alternative / Backup (If the First Plan Fails)
- **Scenario A — You have a confirmed real-name booking:**
  - Present your original passport and booking confirmation to official park staff.
  - Ask for the manual verification point if necessary.
  - Confirm that your booking matches the entrance, date and time slot.
  - Do not purchase another ticket simply because an automatic gate cannot read your passport.

- **Scenario B — You do NOT have a booking:**
  - Move away from touts and approach an identifiable official ticket office or visitor service desk.
  - Ask whether same-day reservations are still available and whether staff can assist with foreign-passport booking.
  - Check the current official booking channel (recommended in 2026 guidance as the WeChat Mini-Program "张家界旅游小助手").
  - If the day's reservation capacity is exhausted, choose another available date or visit another attraction. Do not assume that an offline counter can override capacity restrictions.
  - For visitor-service enquiries or complaints, contact: **0744-5712345**. Ask the operator to direct you to ticketing or passport-verification assistance.

---

## Safety / Don't Do
- **NEVER** buy tickets from scalpers or unofficial resellers.
- **NEVER** pay someone who promises guaranteed entry through an unofficial channel.
- Do not assume that an automatic face-recognition gate will accept a foreign passport booking.
- Do not assume that a booking for one entrance is valid at another entrance.
- Do not buy a second ticket before checking whether your existing booking is valid.
- Do not assume that a sold-out reservation allocation can be bypassed by purchasing at the gate.
- Keep your original passport and booking confirmation secure.

- **Level 1 (Official Operational Guidance):** Zhangjiajie National Forest Park's 2026 visitor guidance (published Sept 29, 2026) confirms: Real-name advance reservations required; Both initial and subsequent entries require reservations; Visitors must use their reserved ticket station and time slot; Unreserved visitors cannot enter.
