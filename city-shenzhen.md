# Action Card: Shenzhen Robotaxis & Huaqiangbei Electronics

## Trigger
You try to download the Pony.ai app for a driverless taxi, but it requires a Chinese phone number. Or you go to Huaqiangbei to buy electronics and get offered "water goods" (refurbished items).

## Immediate Action
For Robotaxi: Do NOT download the app. Open WeChat and search for the Mini-Program "小马智行" (PonyPilot). Tap "Agree and Login with WeChat" to bypass the phone number wall.
For Electronics: Walk away if they offer "Water Goods" (水货). Go to official brand stores (DJI, Huawei) or reputable malls.

## Show This / Action
If bargaining at Huaqiangbei: "老板，这个有保修吗？能开发票吗？我要国行原封的。" (Boss, does this have a warranty? Can you issue a Fapiao? I want the official sealed domestic version.)

## Safety / Don't Do
If crossing to Hong Kong via high-speed rail from Futian to West Kowloon, do NOT use the automatic gates. Find the "Manual Channel" (人工通道) on the far left. Carry your HK entry permit.

## Alternative
If you need a doctor, go to Shekou International Clinic (English-speaking, phone: 0755-2669-1188). If the robotaxi fails, just use a normal DiDi.
