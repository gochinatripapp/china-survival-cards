# Action Card: Amap Navigation & 8D City Rescue

## Trigger
You are using Google Maps and realize it's completely blind in China. You download Amap (高德地图), but the blue dot is spinning, or it tells you to walk through a wall in a city like Chongqing.

## Immediate Action
Do NOT trust the flat 2D map in dense cities. If the destination is close but requires a massive detour, turn on AR Walking Navigation (AR实景导航) and point your camera at the street.

## Show This / Action
To find a hospital in an emergency, do NOT search "Hospital". Search "急诊" (Emergency) or "三甲医院" (Top-Tier Hospital). Check the entrance info to make sure you go to the Emergency gate, not the outpatient gate.

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT use Amap's built-in taxi feature. Use DiDi instead (the bilingual chat prevents cancellation deadlocks). If you are lost in a huge subway station, check the app's exit list (A, B, C, D) and look for landmark clues (e.g., "Exit B: Starbucks") before going up the stairs.

## Alternative
If Amap completely fails, show a local this full-screen card: "你好，我迷路了，能帮我指一下方向吗？" (Hello, I am lost, can you point me in the right direction?).

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Chongqing 8D terrain and Shanghai subway systems.
- **Applies To:** All major cities in China.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/amap-guide.html)
