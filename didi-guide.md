# Action Card: DiDi "Phone Call" & Ride-Hailing Deadlock

## Trigger
You booked a DiDi ride. The driver calls your phone, but you don't speak Chinese. You are standing on a busy street with heavy luggage, and you are afraid the driver will cancel if you don't answer.

## Immediate Action
1. It is usually better to hang up than to panic on a call. 
2. Open the DiDi app and check the driver's license plate and car model.
3. Tap the in-app Chat Bubble (it auto-translates English to Chinese) to communicate your location.

## Alternative / Backup
If the driver cancels or you cannot find the car:
- Confirm the ride is officially cancelled in the app before booking another one.
- Move to a safe, well-lit location (like a hotel lobby or a main street) before hailing a physical taxi as a backup.

## Show This (Friendly Reminder)
**For the in-app chat (type in English, the app translates):**
"I am at the pin. I am wearing a [color] jacket. I have luggage. Please turn on your hazard lights."

**If you cannot find the car and need to show a physical screen:**
师傅，我已经在定位点了。请问您的车具体在哪里？
*(English: Master, I am already at the pin. Where exactly is your car?)*

## Safety / Don't Do
- **PIN Safety:** Only share the ride-specific 4-digit trip PIN after verifying the plate and sitting inside. NEVER share an SMS login code or account security code with anyone.
- **Financial Safety:** Confirm cancellation before booking another ride.
- **Code Safety:** Do NOT get into an unmarked car that pulls up without confirming the plate number.
