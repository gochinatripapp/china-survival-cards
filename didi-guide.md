# Action Card: DiDi "Phone Call" Cancellation Nightmare

## Trigger
You call a DiDi. The driver accepts but calls your phone. You don't speak Chinese, panic, and hang up. The driver cancels the ride. You are stranded.

## Immediate Action
Do NOT answer the phone. Hang up immediately. Do not try to speak Chinese over the phone.

## Show This / Action
Open the DiDi in-app chat bubble. Type in English: "I am at the pin. What store is near you? I am wearing [color]. If you can't find me in 2 minutes, let's cancel." The app auto-translates it to Chinese for the driver.

## Safety / Don't Do
NEVER give the driver the 4-digit phone code until you are fully seated inside the car with the door closed. This code is the platform's safety mechanism to confirm you have boarded.

## Alternative
If the driver cancels, or you cannot find the pin, walk to the main road and hail a physical street taxi. Show them the Chinese address on your phone.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Chongqing 8D terrain and Guangzhou.
- **Applies To:** Tier-1 and Tier-2 city centers.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/didi-guide.html)
