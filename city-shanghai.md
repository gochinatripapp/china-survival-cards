# Action Card: Shanghai Bund DiDi Escape & Tailor Negotiation

## Trigger
You are leaving The Bund at 8:30 PM, the crowd is insane, and your DiDi driver cannot get close because the roads are pedestrian-only. Or you are at the South Bund Fabric Market and don't know how to negotiate.

## Immediate Action
For The Bund: Do NOT stand on East Nanjing Road. Walk to Suzhou Creek (Waibaidu Bridge) where cars can pull over.
For Tailoring: Always cut the initial price in half. Ask directly: "Can you ship this internationally?"

## Show This / Action
For the tailor: "你好，我带了参考图。请确认一下，这个价格是包含全套定制的吗？你们可以直接寄国际快递吗？" (Hello, I brought a reference image. Does this price include full customization? Can you ship internationally?)

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT eat Benbang cuisine at tourist traps like Yu Garden. Go to Yuan Yuan (圆苑) and ask for "less sweet, less oil". Do not use cash for the metro; use the Alipay Transport QR code.

## Alternative
If you need a doctor, go to Shanghai United Family Hospital (24/7 English hotline: 021-2216-3900). If the tailor refuses to ship, there is an official China Post counter nearby.
