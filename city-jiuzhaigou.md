# Action Card: Jiuzhaigou Time-Slot Lockout & Altitude Sickness

## Trigger
You arrive at the gate at 11:00 AM but your ticket says "08:00-10:00", or you start feeling dizzy and nauseous at Five Flower Lake (3,100 meters).

## Immediate Action
For Tickets: The park opens at 08:00. Last entry is 14:00. You must enter within your booked slot. Book 5-7 days in advance via WeChat "Abazhou Tourism" (阿坝旅游网).
For Altitude: Do NOT push through it. Go to the nearest bus stop and take the eco-bus DOWN to a lower altitude. Buy an oxygen can (20-30 RMB at the gate).

## Show This / Action
If you missed your time slot, go to the Ticket Office and show this: "你好，我是刚刚到达的游客，因为交通原因错过了预约时段，请问现在还能进去吗？谢谢！" (Hello, I just arrived and missed my slot due to traffic. Can I enter now? Thanks!)

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT visit Huanglong (3,900m) on Day 1. Do NOT drink alcohol or run for photos on your first day. Do not buy "wild herbs" or "snow lotus" from gate vendors (99% fake).

## Alternative
If you miss your slot and are denied entry, visit the Zhongcha Valley or the Jiuzhaigou County area. For real yak meat, go to Zhangzha Town and find Zang Yage (藏雅阁) or Lao Ma Jia (老马家).
