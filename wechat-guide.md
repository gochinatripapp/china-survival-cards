# Action Card: WeChat Registration & Payment Deadlocks

## Trigger
You are at a hotel front desk or trying to pay at a convenience store. Your WeChat registration is stuck on "Verify with a friend", or the card binding fails due to risk control.

## Immediate Action
Do NOT buy fake verification services online. Tap "Verify with Bank Card" during registration. If that option fails, ask your hotel front desk or airport transfer driver to scan your QR code.

## Show This / Action
For hotel deposits (e.g., 3,000 RMB), do NOT use WeChat. Hand the front desk your physical Visa/Mastercard for a pre-authorization hold to bypass the 3% foreign card fee.

## Safety / Don't Do
Foreign cards CANNOT send money or "Red Packets" to individuals on WeChat. Attempting to do so will trigger a severe risk-control freeze. Use WeChat only to scan merchant QR codes.

## Alternative
If your account gets frozen, switch to Alipay for all payments. Alipay's foreign card binding is much more stable than WeChat.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou & Shanghai.
- **Applies To:** All foreign passport holders.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/wechat-guide.html)
