# Action Card: China Food Delivery & QR Ordering

## Trigger
You order Meituan/Xiaoxiang delivery to your hotel, but the rider calls you in Chinese. Or you sit at a restaurant and realize there is no paper menu, only a QR code on the table.

## Immediate Action
Do NOT answer the delivery phone. Open the app's chat feature. Type in English: "I am a foreigner, do not call. Please leave it at the front desk." The app will auto-translate.
For restaurants: Scan the table QR code using WeChat or Alipay. Most menus have pictures; simply point to the picture to order.

## Show This / Action
If you need to tell a restaurant about a severe allergy, do NOT rely on voice translation. Show them this full-screen card:
"我对花生/海鲜严重过敏，吃了一口就会休克！请千万不要放花生，也不要用炒过花生的锅！" (I am severely allergic to peanuts/seafood. I will go into shock! Please do not use peanuts or the same wok!)

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT trust the "mild" spice level in China. Ask for "Yuan Yang Guo" (split pot) if eating hotpot. Do not pay over 200 RMB by scanning the merchant's static code; hand them your physical card to avoid the 3% fee.

## Alternative
If the rider keeps calling, let it ring. The food will eventually be left at the lobby front desk or a smart locker (look for the 取餐 locker screen). If your food is stolen, request a refund in the app.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou & Chengdu.
- **Applies To:** All major cities.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/food-delivery.html)
