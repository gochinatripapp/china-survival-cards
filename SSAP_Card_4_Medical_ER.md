# SURVIVAL ACTION CARD 04: Medical / Emergency Room Rescue

**Version:** 2.3.2
**Category:** Health & Safety / Emergency

### Mandatory Metadata
- **Evidence Type:** Official Policy + Human Field Observation
- **Applies To:** Foreign travelers experiencing a medical emergency in mainland China.
- **Last Verified:** November 2026
- **Known Limitations:** This is a navigation guide, not medical advice. Hospital procedures and English support vary by city. **Never delay urgent medical care to save money or compare prices.**

---

## 1. TRIGGER (When This Happens)
You or a companion experience severe symptoms (high fever, dehydration, injury). You are unsure how to find a hospital, communicate your medical history, or handle costs.

---

## 2. THE CHOICE (Big Picture Accounting)
In a medical emergency, financial optimization stops. Life and health come first.
- **Priority: Emergency Assessment:** Do not spend an hour comparing hospital prices. Go to the nearest 3A hospital's Emergency Department (急诊科) or call 120.
- **Priority: Dignity & Communication:** Bring your physical passport. Use our bilingual cards to explain symptoms, allergies, and medications accurately.

---

## 3. IMMEDIATE ACTION
1. **Call 120 (if severe) or take a taxi to the ER:** If you can travel, hail a taxi and show them the ER script below.
2. **The 4-Step ER Flow:** Register (挂号) with your passport → Triage (分诊) → Pay for tests (先付费后检查) → Get medicine (拿药).
3. **Request a Fapiao (发票):** Ask for the official receipt for insurance claims.

---

## 4. SHOW THIS (Full-Screen Chinese Cards)

**A. Taxi to the ER:**
> 师傅，我生病了，需要马上就医。请带我去最近的三甲医院急诊科，谢谢！
> *(Master, I am sick and need medical attention immediately. Please take me to the nearest top-tier hospital's emergency department. Thank you!)*

**B. Show the Doctor (Symptoms & Allergies):**
> 医生您好，我不会说中文。我的症状是：[发热/呕吐/腹泻/剧烈疼痛]。
> 我从 [日期] 开始感到不适。
> 我对以下药物过敏：[青霉素/头孢/磺胺等]。
> 我目前正在服用以下药物：[药物名称]。
> *(Hello Doctor, I do not speak Chinese. My symptoms are: [fever/vomiting/diarrhea/severe pain]. I started feeling unwell on [date]. I am allergic to: [penicillin/cephalosporin/etc.]. I am currently taking: [medication name].)*

---

## 5. SAFETY / DON'T DO
- **NEVER** delay emergency treatment to compare hospital prices.
- **NEVER** demand an IV drip without the doctor's assessment. Trust professional clinical judgment.
- **NEVER** rely solely on machine translation for drug allergies. Use this card.
