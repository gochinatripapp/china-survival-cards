# SURVIVAL ACTION CARD 04: Medical / Emergency Room Rescue

**Version:** 2.3.0
**Last updated:** November 2026
**Category:** Health & Safety / Emergency
**Core Philosophy:** Life First. 生命安全优先，明明白白就医。

---

## 1. TRIGGER (When This Happens)
You or a travel companion suddenly experience severe symptoms (high fever, dehydration, vomiting, injury) in mainland China. You are unsure how to find a hospital, communicate your medical history, or handle the costs.

---

## 2. THE CHOICE (Big Picture Accounting)
In a medical emergency, financial optimization is no longer your priority. Life and health come first.
- **Priority: Emergency Assessment:** Do not spend an hour comparing hospital prices before seeking medical assessment. Go to the nearest 3A hospital's Emergency Department (急诊科) or call 120.
- **Priority: Dignity & Communication:** Bring your physical passport. We provide full-screen bilingual cards to help you explain your symptoms, allergies, and medications accurately. 

---

## 3. IMMEDIATE ACTION
1. **Call 120 (if severe) or take a taxi to the ER:** If you cannot travel safely, call 120. If you can, hail a physical taxi and show them the ER script below.
2. **Prepare Your Identity:** Carry your physical passport. It is required for hospital registration.
3. **The 4-Step ER Flow:** Register (挂号) with your passport → Triage (分诊) by a nurse → Pay for tests (先付费后检查) → Get medicine (拿药).
4. **Request a Fapiao (发票):** Always ask for the official receipt for your insurance claims.

---

## 4. SHOW THIS (Full-Screen Chinese Cards)

**A. Taxi to the ER:**
> 师傅，我生病了，需要马上就医。请带我去最近的三甲医院急诊科，谢谢！
> *(Master, I am sick and need medical attention immediately. Please take me to the nearest top-tier hospital's emergency department. Thank you!)*

**B. Show the Doctor (Symptoms & Allergies):**
> 医生您好，我不会说中文。我的症状是：[发热/呕吐/腹泻/剧烈疼痛]。
> 我从 [日期] 开始感到不适。
> 我对以下药物过敏：[青霉素/头孢/磺胺等]。
> 我目前正在服用以下药物：[药物名称]。
> *(Hello Doctor, I do not speak Chinese. My symptoms are: [fever/vomiting/diarrhea/severe pain]. I started feeling unwell on [date]. I am allergic to: [penicillin/cephalosporin/etc.]. I am currently taking: [medication name].)*

**C. Asking for the Bill & Receipt:**
> 请问在哪里交费？我需要正规发票用于保险报销，谢谢！
> *(Where do I pay? I need the official invoice/receipt for insurance claims. Thank you!)*

---

## 5. SAFETY / DON'T DO
- **NEVER** delay emergency treatment to compare hospital prices.
- **NEVER** demand an IV drip without the doctor's assessment. Trust professional clinical judgment.
- **NEVER** rely solely on machine translation for drug allergies. Use this card.
- **NEVER** assume a hotel receptionist can replace a medical assessment.
