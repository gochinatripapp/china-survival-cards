# Action Card: China eSIM & Network Setup

## Trigger
You land in China, turn on your phone, and see a 5G signal, but Google, WhatsApp, and Instagram won't load. Or it says "No Service".

## Immediate Action
Turn off Data Roaming on your home SIM card to avoid a massive bill. Set your pre-installed international eSIM as the Primary Data Line, and turn its Data Roaming ON.

## Show This / Action
If it says "No Service", manually enter the APN code in your phone settings (e.g., `cmnet` for Trip.com eSIM, `globaldata` for Airalo, `nomad` for Nomad).

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT buy a local Chinese SIM card at the airport. Local SIMs are behind the Great Firewall and block Western apps. You must buy an international eSIM before you fly.

## Alternative
If your eSIM completely fails, look for the airport's self-service kiosks where you can scan your physical passport to print a Wi-Fi password.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou (CAN) and Shanghai (PVG).
- **Applies To:** Tourists needing access to Western apps and Google Maps.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/network.html)
