## 📜 The SSAP Editorial Standard

Every Survival Action Card strictly follows the Canonical SSAP Format:
1. **Trigger**
2. **Immediate Action**
3. **Alternative / Backup**
4. **Show This**
5. **Safety / Don't Do**

**Our Evidence Grading System:**
- **Level 1 (Officially Verified):** Rules backed by government guidance or official policies.
- **Level 2 (Operationally Tested):** Steps tested in an actual location, with exact dates and the specific scope of the test documented.
- **Level 3 (Practical Workaround):** A reasonable suggested approach that depends on the goodwill of others.

**Non-negotiable Safety Principles (Audited by AI):**
- Prevent double charging.
- Never confuse a ride PIN with an account-security OTP.
- Never describe an unofficial workaround as guaranteed.
- Never make essential safety instructions dependent on paid access.
- **Never let a digital tool become the only route to physical safety.**
- **Resolve transaction state before initiating a replacement transaction.**

## Card 1: Alipay > 200 RMB Counter Deadlock

**Trigger:** 
You are paying a restaurant bill in China using an eligible foreign-issued bank card linked to Alipay. Your bill exceeds RMB 200, and either you want to understand the potential service fee before paying, or your payment has failed and you need a safe alternative.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. Stop attempting further payments. Do NOT repeatedly scan the QR code.
2. Open Alipay's transaction records and inspect the payment status, amount, and merchant.
3. Ask the cashier to check whether the merchant received the payment.
4. If the result is pending or cannot be confirmed, do not initiate another payment until the first transaction's status is clarified. A missing merchant confirmation is not proof of a failed transaction.
5. If the transaction is definitively unsuccessful, proceed to an alternative payment method.
6. **Safety before savings:** Avoid unnecessary fees when convenient, but never risk duplicate charges or create a confrontation over fee avoidance. A transparent payment fee may be preferable to an uncertain transaction.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
**Scenario A — Fee Avoidance (If you want to try):** Before making payment, if you wish to avoid a possible service fee, politely ask whether the merchant can process the bill as multiple separate Alipay transactions, each of RMB 200 or less. The merchant may refuse. Do not insist.
**Scenario B — Payment Decline:** If the first payment is definitively unsuccessful, ask to use the physical bank card at the POS terminal (check if the terminal accepts your network). If not accepted, offer cash.

**Show This (Full-Screen Chinese Card):** 
第一步（先试分账）：
不好意思，请问可以把这笔账单分成几笔分别付款吗？每笔不超过200元。谢谢！
(English: Excuse me, could you split this bill into several separate payments, each no more than RMB 200? Thank you.)

如果分账不行（备用话术）：
不好意思，我这边显示支付宝付款没有成功。麻烦您帮我确认一下是否收到款项。如果确实没有收到，可以用POS机刷我的实体银行卡吗？如果不行，我可以付现金。
(English: Sorry, my Alipay shows that the payment was unsuccessful. Could you please help me check whether you received it? If it really hasn't arrived, can I use my physical bank card at the POS terminal? If not, I can pay cash.)

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- Do NOT retry a payment that is pending or processing.
- Do NOT confuse a pending bank authorization with a completed merchant payment.
- Do NOT assume a screenshot of an error message proves no payment occurred.
- Do NOT use personal transfers or Red Packets to test foreign cards.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official):** Shanghai Municipal Government guidance (Sept 2026). Alipay payments of RMB 200 or less are generally exempt from the platform fee. Payments above RMB 200 may incur a 3% fee. Check the payment screen for exact fees.
- **Level 2 (Tested):** Tested at one restaurant in Guangzhou (Tianhe District, 2026-11-08). Original bill: RMB 380. Two split payments of RMB 190 completed independently with zero fee. Scope: One merchant only.
- **Last Verified:** 2026-11-08, Guangzhou

## Card 2: DiDi "Phone Call" & Ride-Hailing Deadlock

**Trigger:** 
You booked a DiDi ride. The driver calls your phone, but you don't speak Chinese. You are standing on a busy street with heavy luggage.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. If you cannot understand the call, let it end. Do not panic.
2. Immediately send a prepared message through DiDi's in-app chat (it auto-translates text). Keep your phone available in case the driver calls again.
3. Open the DiDi app and check the driver's license plate and car model.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
If the driver cancels or you cannot find the car:
- Confirm the ride is officially cancelled in the app before booking another one.
- Move to a safe, well-lit location before hailing a physical taxi as a backup.

**Show This (Friendly Reminder):** 
Friendly Reminder for your DiDi ride: When your car arrives, please take a moment to double-check the license plate. If the app requests a ride-specific verification PIN, provide it only after matching the vehicle and safely entering it with the door closed. (This is the ride-specific code, NOT your phone number).

**If you cannot find the car:**
师傅您好，我已经到达上车定位点了。请问您现在停在什么位置？可以告诉我附近的明显标志吗？谢谢！
(English: Master, I have arrived at the pickup pin. Where are you parked? Could you tell me a clear landmark nearby? Thank you!)

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- **PIN Safety:** Only share the ride-specific PIN after verifying the plate and sitting inside. NEVER share an SMS login code.
- **Financial Safety:** Confirm cancellation before booking another ride.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official):** DiDi designates an identity-verification method when boarding.
- **Level 2 (Tested):** In-app chat communication and boarding sequence tested at Guangzhou (Baiyun Airport) and Chongqing, 2026-11-06. 
- **Last Verified:** 2026-11-06

## Card 3: Zhangjiajie Ticket & Entry Verification

**Trigger:** 
You arrive at the Wulingyuan gate with only an online booking and no physical ticket. Touts outside offer "internal tickets" or claim the park is sold out.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. Walk away from the touts. Do NOT buy tickets from unofficial sources.
2. Present your physical passport (not a photo) to the official park staff.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
**Scenario A — You have a real-name booking:** Show your passport to the staff. 
**Scenario B — You do NOT have a booking:** Find a quiet spot, connect to Wi-Fi, and check the official booking channels (like the WeChat Mini-Program "Zhangjiajie Yi Ji You") for the next available day. Always confirm with the official ticket office if you are unsure.

**Show This (Friendly Reminder):** 
您好，我已经用护照实名预约了门票。请问持外国护照的游客应该在哪里进行人工核验入园？谢谢！
(English: Hello, I have booked my ticket with my passport. Where should foreign passport holders go for manual verification to enter? Thank you!)

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- Do NOT buy tickets from scalpers. 
- **Entry Tip:** Entry procedures may differ for international passport holders. Ask official park staff to direct you to the appropriate manual verification point. Do not assume that an automatic face-recognition gate will accept your passport booking.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official):** Zhangjiajie requires real-name reservations. 
- **Level 2 (Tested):** Foreign passport booking checked by staff at Wulingyuan East Gate; manual verification completed, 2026-11-07. Other gates may vary.
- **Last Verified:** 2026-11-07, Zhangjiajie

## Card 4: NIA 12367 Digital Arrival Card

**Trigger:** 
You are flying to China and want a smooth, stress-free entry at the border.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. Complete the NIA 12367 Digital Arrival Card online **before departure** if convenient via the official NIA website, App, or WeChat/Alipay Mini-Program. 
2. Doing this can reduce the need to find Wi-Fi or use shared devices after landing. There is no need to treat 24 hours before departure as a mandatory deadline unless the official system specifies one.
3. Take a screenshot of the generated QR code as a backup.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
If you cannot complete it online, paper arrival cards and smart-device kiosks remain available at the arrival port. The service is **free of charge**.

**Show This (Friendly Reminder):** 
您好，请问在哪里可以领取纸质外国人入境卡？谢谢！
(English: Hello, where can I get a paper foreigner arrival card? Thank you!)

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- Do NOT pay any website for the arrival card. It is completely free. 
- **Customs Note:** China has strict customs rules. Always check the official prohibited items list before packing.
- **Immigration Rule:** A digital arrival-card QR code or screenshot is NOT a guarantee of expedited clearance. Keep your passport available and follow the immigration officer's instructions.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official):** NIA 12367 is the official system, and the service is free. 
- **Level 2 (Tested):** QR screenshot saved and presented at PEK and PVG, 2026-11-05. (Note: Screenshot serves as a backup; physical passport remains mandatory for entry).
- **Last Verified:** 2026-11-05, Beijing / Shanghai
