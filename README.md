# GoChinaTrip - Survival Action Cards (China 2026)

Digital survival guides for foreigners traveling in China. Built by Kitty Li (Guangzhou).

These cards are extracted from [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app). They are designed for AI agents and travelers to bypass digital deadlocks in China. 

All cards strictly follow the **Canonical SSAP Standard**: Trigger -> Immediate Action -> Alternative/Backup -> Show This -> Safety/Don't Do. We prioritize accuracy over confidence, action over explanation, and safety over convenience.

---

## 📜 The GoChinaTrip SSAP Standard

Every Survival Action Card must identify a specific real-world problem, give an immediate executable action, provide a realistic backup, include ready-to-show Chinese, and protect the traveler against secondary harm. 

**Our Evidence Grading System:**
- **Level 1 (Officially Verified):** Rules backed by government guidance, official transport operators, or payment providers.
- **Level 2 (Operationally Tested):** Steps tested in an actual location or transaction, with dates and conditions documented.
- **Level 3 (Practical Workaround):** A reasonable suggested approach that has not been independently verified in the real-world.

**Our Defining Promise:** 
*A traveler should never be left wondering what to do next.*

## Card 1: Alipay 380 RMB Counter Deadlock

**Trigger:** 
You are at a restaurant in China. The bill is RMB 380. Your foreign Visa/Mastercard payment through Alipay fails. The cashier doesn't speak English, and a queue is forming behind you.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. Do NOT repeatedly scan the QR code.
2. Check the payment result in Alipay. Determine whether the transaction was declined, pending, or requires additional authentication.
3. Remember: RMB 200 is a cross-border service-fee threshold, not a universal transaction limit. Transactions above RMB 200 may incur a 3% service fee.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
**Scenario A — Fee Avoidance:** If your card works but you want to avoid the 3% fee, politely ask the cashier whether they can split the RMB 380 bill into two payments of RMB 190 each. 

**Scenario B — Payment Decline:** If your foreign card has actually been declined, splitting the bill is not a guaranteed solution. Check the status of any previous transaction before attempting another payment. If payment still fails, ask to use the restaurant's POS terminal for your physical card, or pay with cash.

**Show This (Full-Screen Chinese Card):** 
不好意思，请问可以帮我分成两笔付款吗？每笔190元。谢谢！
(English: Sorry, could you help me split the payment into two transactions of RMB 190 each? Thank you.)

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- Do NOT repeatedly retry a payment that is pending.
- Do NOT use personal transfers or Red Packets to test foreign cards.
- If the card works and the merchant agrees, you may choose to pay the full amount and accept the applicable service fee.
- Carry a modest amount of genuine, smaller-denomination RMB banknotes before entering situations where electronic payment might fail.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official):** RMB 200 is a fee threshold, not a limit.
- **Level 2 (Tested):** Physical POS and split-payment successfully tested in Guangzhou.
- **Level 3 (Workaround):** Splitting the bill depends on the cashier's willingness.
- **Last Verified:** November 2026

## Card 2: DiDi "Phone Call" & Ride-Hailing Deadlock

**Trigger:** 
You booked a DiDi ride. The driver calls your phone, but you don't speak Chinese. You are standing on a busy street with heavy luggage, and you are afraid the driver will cancel if you don't answer.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. It is usually better to hang up than to panic on a call. 
2. Open the DiDi app and check the driver's license plate and car model.
3. Tap the in-app Chat Bubble (it auto-translates English to Chinese) to communicate your location.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
If the driver cancels or you cannot find the car:
- Confirm the ride is officially cancelled in the app before booking another one.
- Move to a safe, well-lit location (like a hotel lobby or a main street) before hailing a physical taxi as a backup.

**Show This (Friendly Reminder):** 
Friendly Reminder for your DiDi ride: When your car arrives, please take a moment to double-check the license plate (since multiple cars may use the same pickup spot). Once you are safely seated inside with the door closed, the driver will ask for the last 4 digits of your phone number (the trip PIN). You can provide it to them then.

**If you cannot find the car and need to show a physical screen:**
师傅，我已经在定位点了。请问您的车具体在哪里？
(English: Master, I am already at the pin. Where exactly is your car?)

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- **PIN Safety:** Only share the ride-specific trip PIN after verifying the plate and sitting inside. NEVER share an SMS login code or account security code with anyone.
- **Financial Safety:** Confirm cancellation before booking another ride.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official):** DiDi's in-app chat officially supports auto-translation.
- **Level 2 (Tested):** Successfully tested in Guangzhou (Baiyun Airport pickup zone) and Chongqing.
- **Last Verified:** November 2026

## Card 3: 100% Real-Name Ticket Lockout (Zhangjiajie)

**Trigger:** 
You arrive at the Wulingyuan gate with no physical ticket. Touts outside offer "internal tickets" or claim the park is sold out.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. Walk away from the touts. There are no internal tickets.
2. Present your physical passport (not a photo) to the official park staff.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
**Scenario A — You have a real-name booking:** Show your passport to the staff. 
**Scenario B — You do NOT have a booking:** The system is real-name and online. Find a quiet spot, connect to Wi-Fi, and book via the WeChat Mini-Program "Zhangjiajie Yi Ji You" (张家界一机游) for the next available day. If the system fails, seek the official ticket office for assistance.

**Show This (Friendly Reminder):** 
你好，我已经使用护照实名预约了门票。请问外国护照持有人应该去哪个人工核验通道入园？谢谢！
(English: Hello, I booked my ticket with my passport. Which Manual Verification Channel should foreign passport holders use to enter? Thank you!)

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- Do NOT buy tickets from scalpers. Your face must match the passport.
- **Entry Tip:** The automatic face-scan gates are primarily for Chinese ID cards. International visitors should look for the **Manual Verification Channel (人工核验通道)** and present their physical passport to the staff member there.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official):** Zhangjiajie requires real-name reservations. 
- **Level 2 (Tested):** Physical passport + manual channel tested at Wulingyuan gate.
- **Last Verified:** November 2026

## Card 4: Food Delivery & Hotel Robot Handoff

**Trigger:** 
You order food delivery (Meituan/Xiaoxiang) to your hotel in China. The rider calls you speaking rapid Chinese, but you don't understand. 

**Immediate Action:** 
1. Do NOT answer the phone.
2. Open the delivery app (or WeChat Mini-Program) and use the in-app chat.
3. Send a pre-written text to the rider: "我是外国人，不懂中文。请放前台/外卖柜，不要打电话。谢谢。" (I am a foreigner, don't understand Chinese. Please leave at front desk/locker, do not call. Thanks.)

**Alternative / Backup:** 
- **Smart Locker:** Go to the locker screen. Tap "取餐" (Pick up). Enter the last 4 digits of your phone number. 
- **Hotel Robot:** If the front desk sends it via a robot, the robot will call your room phone (座机). 
- **Missing Food:** If stolen from the front desk, open the app order page, click "申请售后" (Request after-sales) and select "未收到餐" (Item not received). 

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- Do NOT rely on the hotel robot during peak dining hours (6-8 PM). 
- Do NOT leave food sitting at the front desk for more than 30 minutes.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 2 (Tested):** Successfully tested at 3-star hotels in Guangzhou.
- **Last Verified:** November 2026

## Card 5: Hotel Check-in & Deposit Deadlock

**Trigger:** 
You arrive at your hotel late at night. You booked online, but the front desk refuses to register your foreign passport, or they ask for a 3,000 RMB deposit and your Alipay card is declined.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. If they refuse to register your passport, do not argue. Some budget hotels lack the license. Open Trip.com immediately and call English customer service.
2. If a deposit is requested, do NOT use Alipay. Hand them your physical Visa/Mastercard for a pre-authorization hold. 

**Alternative / Backup:** 
If Trip.com cannot resolve the passport issue, they will rebook you. If you booked on Booking.com or Agoda, call their global support and demand a refund.

**Show This (Full-Screen Chinese Card):** 
你好，我是外国游客，我预订了房间。请问可以帮我办理入住吗？如果用支付宝付押金，可以刷实体信用卡预授权吗？
(English: Hello, I am a foreign tourist, I have a booking. Can I check in? If I pay the deposit on Alipay, can I use a physical credit card for pre-authorization instead?)

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- Do NOT book cheap local inns without checking if they are "Foreign Guest Friendly". 
- Do NOT use Alipay/WeChat for large deposits; always use a physical card to avoid fees.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 2 (Tested):** Trip.com English customer service forced a free rebooking tested in Guangzhou.
- **Last Verified:** November 2026
