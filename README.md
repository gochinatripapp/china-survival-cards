## Card 1: Alipay 380 RMB Counter Deadlock

**Trigger:** Foreigner finishes a 380 RMB meal. The foreign Visa card fails. Cashier doesn't speak English, queue is forming.

**Immediate Action:** Politely ask the cashier to split the bill into two scans, each under 200 RMB.

**Show This Chinese:** 不好意思，请问能不能麻烦您帮我分两笔扫？十分感谢！

**Safety / Don't Do:** Do not use personal transfers or red packets to test foreign cards. Foreign cards are blocked from this, and failed attempts may trigger additional account-verification or risk-control freezes.

**Alternative:** If they refuse, accept gracefully. Show Alipay payment code and let them scan the full amount (accepting the 3% cross-border fee).

**Evidence:** Last Verified: Oct 2026. Tested in Guangzhou. Applies to Foreign Guest Friendly merchants.

## Card 2: DiDi "Phone Call" Cancellation Nightmare

**Trigger:** DiDi driver accepts the ride but calls you. You don't speak Chinese, the driver cancels.

**Immediate Action:** Hang up the phone. Do not try to speak Chinese.

**Show This / Action:** Open the DiDi in-app chat. Type in English: "I am at the pin. What store is near you? I am wearing [color]. If you can't find me in 2 minutes, let's cancel." The app auto-translates.

**Safety / Don't Do:** Never give the driver your 4-digit phone code until you are fully seated inside the car with the door closed.

**Alternative:** If stuck, cancel and walk to the main road to hail a physical taxi.

**Evidence:** Last Verified: Oct 2026. Tested in Chongqing 8D terrain and Guangzhou.

## Card 3: 100% Real-Name Ticket Lockout (Zhangjiajie)

**Trigger:** Arriving at Wulingyuan gate with no ticket. Touts offer "internal tickets".

**Immediate Action:** Walk away. There are no internal tickets.

**Show This Chinese:** 你好，我已用护照在「张家界一机游」上实名预约了门票，请问刷脸通道在哪里？谢谢！

**Safety / Don't Do:** 100% real-name, online-only reservation system. No windows. Ignore scalpers.

**Alternative:** Book 2-7 days ahead via WeChat Mini-Program "Zhangjiajie Yi Ji You".

**Evidence:** Last Verified: Oct 2026. Price: 228 RMB main ticket (4-day validity).
