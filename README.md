## 📜 The SSAP Editorial Standard

Every Survival Action Card strictly follows the Canonical SSAP Format:
1. **Trigger**
2. **Immediate Action**
3. **Alternative / Backup**
4. **Show This**
5. **Safety / Don't Do**

**Our Evidence Grading System:**
- **Level 1 (Officially Verified):** Rules backed by government guidance or official policies.
- **Level 2 (Operationally Tested):** Steps tested in an actual location, with exact dates and the specific scope of the test documented.
- **Level 3 (Practical Workaround):** A reasonable suggested approach that depends on the goodwill of others.

**Non-negotiable Safety Principles (Audited by AI):**
- **Resolve transaction state before initiating a replacement transaction.** (Prevent duplicate payments)
- **Verify Before Boarding.** (Establish vehicle identity before entering)
- **Match Capacity Before Booking.** (Confirm passenger and luggage requirements before selecting a vehicle)
- **Preparation is not obligation. Submission is not permission. Digital convenience is not physical dependency.**
- Never confuse a ride PIN with an account-security OTP.
- Never describe an unofficial workaround as guaranteed.
- Never make essential safety instructions dependent on paid access.
- **Never let a digital tool become the only route to physical safety.**

## Card 1: Alipay > 200 RMB Counter Deadlock

**Trigger:** 
You are paying a restaurant bill in China using an eligible foreign-issued bank card linked to Alipay. Your bill exceeds RMB 200, and either you want to understand the potential service fee before paying, or your payment has failed and you need a safe alternative.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. Stop attempting further payments. Do NOT repeatedly scan the QR code.
2. Open Alipay's transaction records and inspect the payment status, amount, and merchant.
3. Ask the cashier to check whether the merchant received the payment.
4. If the result is pending or cannot be confirmed, do not initiate another payment until the first transaction's status is clarified. A missing merchant confirmation is not proof of a failed transaction.
5. If the transaction is definitively unsuccessful, proceed to an alternative payment method.
6. **Safety before savings:** Avoid unnecessary fees when convenient, but never risk duplicate charges or create a confrontation over fee avoidance. A transparent payment fee may be preferable to an uncertain transaction.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
**Scenario A — Fee Avoidance (If you want to try):** Before making payment, if you wish to avoid a possible service fee, politely ask whether the merchant can process the bill as multiple separate Alipay transactions, each of RMB 200 or less. The merchant may refuse. Do not insist.
**Scenario B — Payment Decline:** If the first payment is definitively unsuccessful, ask to use the physical bank card at the POS terminal (check if the terminal accepts your network). If not accepted, offer cash.

**Show This (Full-Screen Chinese Card):** 
第一步（先试分账）：
不好意思，请问可以把这笔账单分成几笔分别付款吗？每笔不超过200元。谢谢！
(English: Excuse me, could you split this bill into several separate payments, each no more than RMB 200? Thank you.)

如果分账不行（备用话术）：
不好意思，我这边显示支付宝付款没有成功。麻烦您帮我确认一下是否收到款项。如果确实没有收到，可以用POS机刷我的实体银行卡吗？如果不行，我可以付现金。
(English: Sorry, my Alipay shows that the payment was unsuccessful. Could you please help me check whether you received it? If it really hasn't arrived, can I use my physical bank card at the POS terminal? If not, I can pay cash.)

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- Do NOT retry a payment that is pending or processing.
- Do NOT confuse a pending bank authorization with a completed merchant payment.
- Do NOT assume a screenshot of an error message proves no payment occurred.
- Do NOT use personal transfers or Red Packets to test foreign cards.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official):** Shanghai Municipal Government guidance (Sept 2026). Alipay payments of RMB 200 or less are generally exempt from the platform fee. Payments above RMB 200 may incur a 3% fee. Check the payment screen for exact fees.
- **Level 2 (Tested):** Tested at one restaurant in Guangzhou (Tianhe District, 2026-11-08). Original bill: RMB 380. Two split payments of RMB 190 completed independently with zero fee. Scope: One merchant only.
- **Last Verified:** 2026-11-08, Guangzhou

## Card 2: DiDi "Phone Call" & Ride-Hailing Deadlock

**Prevention First (Before You Book):** 
If you are traveling to or from an airport with multiple or oversized suitcases, elderly passengers, or children, check the vehicle's passenger and luggage capacity before confirming your booking.
- Do not assume a standard DiDi Express vehicle can accommodate your entire group and luggage.
- Consider DiDi Premier (专车), an eligible larger vehicle category, or a pre-booked Trip.com airport transfer.
- If booking through Trip.com, check the specific vehicle's confirmed passenger and luggage allowance. A seven-seater does not automatically guarantee sufficient luggage space.
- Request luggage assistance or other special services only when they are explicitly included or confirmed in your booking.
The goal is to select a vehicle that can safely accommodate every passenger and every suitcase before the journey begins.

**Trigger:** 
You have booked a DiDi ride in mainland China. The driver calls in Mandarin, you cannot communicate verbally, or you cannot identify the vehicle at the pickup point. You may be carrying luggage and need to confirm the correct vehicle and driver before boarding.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. Let an unintelligible call end; remain calm. Do not cross traffic or walk into the road while looking at your phone.
2. Move to a safe, precise pickup position (e.g., visible landmark, designated pickup zone) before the driver arrives.
3. Open the active DiDi order. Check the driver's information and all available identification details: **license plate and vehicle make/model, plus vehicle colour and driver's photograph where displayed.**
4. Send a short message via the DiDi in-app chat (which supports auto-translation).
5. When the car arrives, match its registration plate and details against the app. **Only board when they match.**
6. After confirming the vehicle identity and entering safely, follow the verification instructions displayed in the active trip.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
- **If the driver cancels:** Check if a cancellation fee applies. Rebook only through the app. **NEVER accept an off-platform journey or pay the driver privately.**
- **If you cannot find the car:** Use the in-app chat first. If it fails, move to a safe, well-lit location to hail a physical taxi from an official stand. Do not enter an unidentified car.
- **If you have luggage:** If the luggage cannot fit safely in the vehicle's designated storage area, do not force it into the passenger cabin or compromise passenger safety. Discuss the problem through the app, and if the vehicle is unsuitable, arrange cancellation through the platform and book a vehicle with confirmed capacity. Check any applicable cancellation fee.

**Show This (Full-Screen Chinese Cards):** 
*(Send the relevant message based on the situation. Do not send all at once.)*

**1. Locating & Identifying:**
师傅您好，我已经到软件显示的上车点了。请问您现在在哪里？附近有什么明显的地标吗？谢谢！
*(Hello, I've arrived at the pickup point shown in the app. Where are you now? Is there a recognizable landmark nearby? Thank you!)*

**2. Describing Yourself:**
师傅您好，我穿着一件蓝色外套，站在上车点旁边。
*(Hello, I'm wearing a blue jacket and standing next to the pickup point.)*

**3. Requesting Hazard Lights:**
师傅您好，可以麻烦您打开双闪灯吗？这样我比较容易找到您的车。谢谢！
*(Hello, could you please turn on your hazard lights? That will make it easier for me to find your car. Thank you!)*

**4. Luggage Coordination (For Express/Standard rides):**
师傅您好，我有三个行李箱，请问后备箱能放得下吗？麻烦您打开一下后备箱，谢谢！
*(Hello, I have three suitcases. Will they fit in the trunk? Could you please help open the trunk? Thank you!)*

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- **Verify Before Boarding:** Never rely on verbal claims to establish vehicle identity. If the plate does not match the active booking, **DO NOT BOARD**, even if the driver knows your name. (Note: Hazard lights help identify a vehicle, but they do not establish that it is the correct vehicle.)
- **Verification Boundary:** Follow the platform's designated verification procedure. NEVER share an SMS login code, account password, or payment password.
- **Off-platform Rule:** Never cancel an active order to arrange a private journey or direct cash payment.
- **Seatbelt & Belongings:** Confirm all bags are loaded, the trunk is closed, and your seatbelt is fastened before departure. Do not place large suitcases on passenger seats.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official):** 
  - Shanghai Municipal Government, "How to use DiDi Chuxing" (Updated July 21, 2026). Confirms driver location, vehicle type/plate display, automatic English translation, and last-four-digit mobile-number verification after entering the vehicle.
  - Beijing Municipal Government, "Taxis & Ride-Hailing" (Published April 11, 2023). Corroborates plate checking, last-four-digit verification, and translated messaging.
  - Trip.com China airport transfer guidance. Advises checking specific vehicle baggage allowances.
- **Level 2 (Tested):** In-app chat translation and boarding verification tested at Guangzhou (Baiyun Airport) and Chongqing, 2026-11-06. Test scope: English app interface, license plate matching, bidirectional translated messaging. Limitations: Verification method and vehicle info displayed may vary by trip category and app version. Luggage capacity depends on the vehicle model.
- **Last Verified:** 2026-11-06 (operational testing; official source review updated November 12, 2026).

## Card 3: Zhangjiajie Ticket & Entry Verification

**Trigger:** 
You arrive at a Zhangjiajie National Forest Park entrance with an online booking but no physical ticket. Alternatively, you discover that you have not made a reservation. Touts nearby offer "internal tickets" or claim the park is sold out.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. Ignore touts offering unofficial tickets, guaranteed entry, or special access.
2. Keep your original physical passport ready. A photograph of your passport is not a substitute for the original identity document when physical verification is required.
3. If you already have a booking, retrieve your confirmation and check:
   - Visitor name and passport number.
   - Payment and booking confirmation status.
   - Reserved admission date and time slot.
   - Reserved entrance/ticket station.
4. Follow the official entrance instructions. If the normal automatic gate cannot verify your foreign passport, ask an official staff member to direct you to manual document verification.
5. If you plan to leave and re-enter the park, check and complete the separate re-entry reservation before leaving the park.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
- **Scenario A — You have a confirmed real-name booking:**
  - Present your original passport and booking confirmation to official park staff.
  - Ask for the manual verification point if necessary.
  - Confirm that your booking matches the entrance, date and time slot.
  - Do not purchase another ticket simply because an automatic gate cannot read your passport.
- **Scenario B — You do NOT have a booking:**
  - Move away from touts and approach an identifiable official ticket office or visitor service desk.
  - Ask whether same-day reservations are still available and whether staff can assist with foreign-passport booking.
  - Check the current official booking channel (recommended in 2026 guidance as the WeChat Mini-Program "张家界旅游小助手").
  - If the day's reservation capacity is exhausted, choose another available date or visit another attraction. Do not assume that an offline counter can override capacity restrictions.
  - For visitor-service enquiries or complaints, contact: 0744-5712345. Ask the operator to direct you to ticketing or passport-verification assistance.

**Show This (Full-Screen Chinese Cards):** 
*(Show the relevant script based on your situation)*

**1. Passport Verification:**
您好，我已经使用外国护照实名预约并购买了门票。请问持外国护照的游客应该在哪里进行人工证件核验和入园？这是我的护照和订单确认信息。麻烦您帮我确认一下，谢谢！
*(English: Hello. I have made a real-name reservation and purchased my ticket using my foreign passport. Where should foreign passport holders go for manual document verification and park entry? Here are my passport and booking confirmation. Could you please help me check them? Thank you!)*

**2. No Reservation:**
您好，我还没有预约门票。请问今天还有余票吗？外国护照可以在哪里实名预约购票？
*(English: Hello. I haven't booked a ticket yet. Are there any tickets available today? Where can foreign passport holders make a real-name reservation and purchase tickets?)*

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- NEVER buy tickets from scalpers or unofficial resellers.
- NEVER pay someone who promises guaranteed entry through an unofficial channel.
- Do not assume that an automatic face-recognition gate will accept a foreign passport booking.
- Do not assume that a booking for one entrance is valid at another entrance.
- Do not buy a second ticket before checking whether your existing booking is valid.
- Do not assume that a sold-out reservation allocation can be bypassed by purchasing at the gate.
- Keep your original passport and booking confirmation secure.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official Operational Guidance):** Zhangjiajie National Forest Park's 2026 visitor guidance (published Sept 29, 2026) confirms: Real-name advance reservations required; Both initial and subsequent entries require reservations; Visitors must use their reserved ticket station and time slot; Unreserved visitors cannot enter. Recommended booking mini-program in the September 29, 2026 park visitor guidance: 张家界旅游小助手.
- **Level 2 (Documented Field Test):** Foreign passport booking checked by staff at Wulingyuan East Gate; manual verification completed, November 7, 2026. Other gates may vary.
- **Last Verified:** November 12, 2026.

## Card 4: NIA 12367 Digital Arrival Card

**Trigger:** 
You are traveling to mainland China and need to submit foreigner arrival information, but have not completed the online arrival card, are unsure whether submission succeeded, or cannot access the digital system. Travelers covered by an official exemption do not need to submit the arrival card.

**Immediate Action:** 
1. Complete the NIA 12367 Digital Arrival Card online **before departure** if convenient via the official NIA website, App, or WeChat/Alipay Mini-Program. 
2. Doing this can reduce the need to find Wi-Fi or use shared devices after landing. There is no need to treat 24 hours before departure as a mandatory deadline unless the official system specifies one.
3. After successful submission, save the confirmation page and generated QR code as an offline screenshot. This is a convenient backup, not a guarantee of acceptance or a replacement for your original passport.

**Alternative / Backup:** 
If you cannot complete the form online before departure, you can complete it at the immigration inspection area upon arrival:
- Scan the official on-site arrival-card QR code using your phone.
- Use an available on-site self-service terminal.
- Request a paper Foreign Arrival Card if you cannot use the digital options.
The service is completely free. Do not assume that you must have completed the form before boarding.

**Show This (Friendly Reminder):** 
您好，我无法提前在线填写外国人入境卡。请问这里有现场自助设备吗？如果无法使用自助设备，请问在哪里可以领取纸质外国人入境卡？谢谢！
*(English: Hello. I was unable to complete the foreigner arrival card online in advance. Are there self-service terminals here? If I cannot use a self-service terminal, where can I get a paper foreigner arrival card? Thank you!)*

**Safety / Don't Do:** 
- NEVER pay a third-party website to submit the arrival card. It is completely free.
- NEVER assume that a screenshot alone guarantees successful submission or entry.
- NEVER treat an arrival-card QR code as a visa or entry permit.
- Keep your original passport accessible.
- Follow immigration officers' instructions.
- Remember that paper arrival cards and on-site digital facilities remain available if online preparation fails.

**Evidence:** 
- **Level 1 (Official):** NIA official announcement (Nov 2025); NIA fraud warning (Dec 2025); Chinese Embassy international-traveler guidance (Oct 2026). Confirms online submission is available, the service is free, and paper/on-site digital alternatives remain.
- **Level 2 (Tested):** QR screenshot saved and presented at PEK and PVG, 2026-11-05. Note: This is a two-airport field test, not a universal acceptance guarantee. Physical passport remains mandatory.
- **Last Policy Check:** 2026-11-12
- **Last Field Test:** 2026-11-05, Beijing / Shanghai
