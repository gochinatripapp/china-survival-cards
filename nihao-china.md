# Action Card: Nihao China Transit & Tax Refund

## Trigger
You are at a subway station and the gate rejects your QR code. Or you bought a 5,000 RMB item and the airport tax refund kiosk rejects your passport.

## Immediate Action
For the subway: Chinese metro stations usually have ZERO cellular signal underground. You MUST open Nihao China on the street, generate the transit QR code, and screenshot it before going down the stairs.

## Show This / Action
If the gate still rejects the screenshot, step out of line. Go to the Customer Service Center (客服中心) and say "Scan fail". They will manually open the gate.
For Tax Refunds: If the kiosk says "Info Mismatch", do NOT walk away. Go to the Manual Refund Counter (人工退税柜台) and show your passport, receipt, and item.

## Safety / Don't Do
Nihao China CANNOT scan personal (P2P) QR codes at street stalls. It only accepts merchant codes. For street food, use Alipay or cash. Do NOT use Alipay for purchases over 200 RMB to avoid the 3% fee.

## Alternative
If you forget to screenshot and have no signal, do not block the turnstile. Walk to the station staff and show them your phone screen; they usually have a master card to let you through.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou Metro, Shanghai Metro, Airport Refund Counters.
- **Applies To:** 43 cities for transit, 29 cities for tax refund.
