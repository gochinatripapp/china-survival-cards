# Action Card: Zhangjiajie 100% Real-Name Ticket Lockout

## Trigger
You arrive at the Wulingyuan gate with no physical ticket. Touts outside offer "internal tickets" or claim the park is sold out.

## Immediate Action
1. Walk away from the touts. There are no internal tickets.
2. Present your physical passport (not a photo) to the official park staff.

## Alternative / Backup
**Scenario A — You have a real-name booking:** 
Show your passport to the staff at the entrance.

**Scenario B — You do NOT have a booking:** 
The system is real-name and online. Find a quiet spot, connect to Wi-Fi, and book via the WeChat Mini-Program "Zhangjiajie Yi Ji You" (张家界一机游) for the next available day. If the system fails, seek the official ticket office for assistance.

## Safety / Don't Do
- Do NOT buy tickets from scalpers. Your face must match the passport.
- **Entry Tip:** The automatic face-scan gates are primarily for Chinese ID cards. International visitors should look for the **Manual Verification Channel (人工核验通道)** and present their physical passport to the staff member there.
- Do NOT carry food in visible plastic bags near Golden Whip Stream (macaques will attack).
