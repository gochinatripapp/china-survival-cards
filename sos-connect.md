# Action Card: Police SOS & Lost Passport in China

## Trigger
You lost your passport in China, or you need to file a police report, but you don't speak Chinese and don't know who to call.

## Immediate Action
Call Police 110, Ambulance 120, Fire 119. If you are at a hotel, go to the front desk first. They can translate and guide emergency services.

## Show This / Action
Show this full-screen card to the police to get your lost passport report:
"警察同志，我的护照丢了。我需要报警回执去大使馆补办。请帮帮我。" (Officer, I lost my passport. I need a police report to get an emergency travel document from my embassy. Please help me.)

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT leave China without resolving this. You need the police report receipt, then contact your embassy for an emergency document, and finally go to the Exit-Entry Administration (出入境管理局) for an exit visa.

## Alternative
If you are alone and cannot speak, send this message to your hotel front desk on WeChat: "你好，我遇到了紧急情况，我不会说中文，能请你们前台帮我打个报警电话吗？" (Hello, I have an emergency. I cannot speak Chinese. Can the front desk help me call the police?)

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou.
- **Applies To:** All major Chinese cities.
