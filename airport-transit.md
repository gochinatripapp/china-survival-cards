# Action Card: China Airport Layover & Free Transit Tours

## Trigger
You have a long layover (8+ hours) in a Chinese airport (like Shanghai Pudong or Guangzhou Baiyun). You are stuck at the gate and bored, or you want to leave the airport but aren't sure about the visa rules.

## Immediate Action
Check if you qualify for the **240-hour transit visa-free policy** (applies to 54 countries). If you have a confirmed onward ticket to a third country, you can leave the airport.

## Show This / Action
Go to the official "Transit Tour Counter" in the international arrivals hall.
- **Guangzhou (CAN)**: Counter opposite Gate 50. Opens at 7:00 AM. Offers a free bus tour (includes lunch) or a free metro tour (includes a 24-hour metro ticket).
- **Shanghai (PVG)**: Gate 7 in the arrivals hall (Eastern Airlines counter). MUST book 12 hours in advance on the Eastern Airlines App. Does NOT include meals.

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT follow signs to the paid city tour desks. You must find the official transit counter. Do NOT try to leave the airport without confirming your visa-free eligibility and keeping your onward boarding pass handy.

## Alternative
If your layover is short or you cannot leave the airport, look for the self-service kiosks where you can scan your physical passport to print a Wi-Fi password. Do not sit at the gate; find a lounge or rest area.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou Baiyun (CAN) & Shanghai Pudong (PVG).
- **Applies To:** Major international airports in China.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/airport-transit-tours.html)
