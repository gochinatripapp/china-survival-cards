# Action Card: Airport Tax Refund & Shipping Rules

## Trigger
You bought items in China and want the 11% VAT tax refund at the airport. The kiosk rejects your passport, or you want to mail the items home.

## Immediate Action
If the automated kiosk rejects your passport, do NOT walk away and lose your money. Go straight to the Manual Refund Counter (人工退税柜台). Hand them your physical passport, the receipt (Fapiao), and the physical item.

## Show This / Action
Show this to the officer: "机器无法识别我的护照，请帮我人工办理退税，谢谢。" (The machine cannot read my passport, please help me process the refund manually.)

## Safety / Don't Do
HARD BOUNDARY: You must choose ONE path.
Path A (Refund): You MUST carry the goods out of China personally. Customs must inspect the physical items.
Path B (Shipping): If you mail the goods home via SF Express or FedEx, you FORFEIT the tax refund entirely. You cannot do both.

## Alternative
Always shop at stores with the "Tax Free" (退税) sign. You must spend at least 200 RMB in a single store on the same day. Ask for the official Fapiao before leaving the store.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Shanghai Pudong (PVG) and Beijing Capital (PEK).
- **Applies To:** 29 cities with tax refund policies.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/nihao-guide.html)
