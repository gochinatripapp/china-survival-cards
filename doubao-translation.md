# Action Card: Doubao AI Translation & Real-World Setups

## Trigger
You need to communicate with a taxi driver or a restaurant waiter, but you have no VPN. You need a translation app that works flawlessly without a VPN in mainland China.

## Immediate Action
Download Doubao (豆包), built by ByteDance. Register with your international number. It works without a VPN. Open the "Simultaneous Interpretation" (同声传译) mode.

## Show This / Action
For taxis: Place the phone flat on the center console between you and the driver. Say "Please take me to this address." Do NOT hold the phone to your ear.
For noisy street vendors: Do NOT use voice translation. Point at the food and show this card: "老板，我要这个，请问多少钱？" (Boss, I want this, how much?).

## Safety / Don't Do
Never use AI to answer live phone calls (the app cannot hear the caller). If a delivery driver calls, hang up and use text-based chat. Do not use voice translation in extremely noisy subway stations or crowded intersections.

## Alternative
If the AI misinterprets a thick local dialect, do not try to force it. Open your phone's screen and show the Chinese characters directly to the person. For hotels, place the phone flat on the counter and ask them to speak into it.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou & Shanghai.
- **Applies To:** Face-to-face communication. Not applicable for live phone calls.
