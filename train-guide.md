# Action Card: China High-Speed Train Gate Bypass

## Trigger
You arrive at a Chinese train station with your passport. You try to scan it at the automatic turnstile, but it flashes a red error and the gate won't open. The queue is building behind you.

## Immediate Action
Do NOT keep scanning. Step out of the way. The turnstile scanners physically cannot read foreign passport chips.

## Show This / Action
Walk to the far left side of the gates and look for the "Manual Channel" (人工通道). Hand your physical passport to the staff member there. They will scan it manually and let you through instantly.

## Safety / Don't Do
Do not buy paper tickets from touts outside. Your passport IS your ticket. Never leave your passport in your checked luggage; keep it on your person at all times.

## Alternative
If you are hungry on the train, do not buy the cold, overpriced food cart meals. Use the 12306 WeChat Mini-Program or Trip.com to order hot food from upcoming stations. Order at least 1 hour in advance to your seat.

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** November 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou South Station, Shanghai Hongqiao Station.
- **Applies To:** All high-speed rail stations in mainland China.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/train-guide.html)
