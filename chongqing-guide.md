# Action Card: Chongqing 8D Navigation Deadlock

## Trigger
You are in Chongqing. Amap shows the destination is 100 meters away, but requires climbing 8 flights of stairs or crossing an overpass with no pedestrian path. The DiDi driver calls and says he is on the 3rd floor.

## Immediate Action
STOP WALKING. Do not trust the 2D map. Turn on AR Walking Navigation in Amap. If you are taking a DiDi, hang up the phone, use the in-app chat to say "I am at the pin. What store is near you?"

## Show This / Action
If you cannot find the driver and it's raining, cancel the ride. Show a physical taxi driver: "师傅，我生病了/迷路了，请带我去[目的地]。" (Master, I am sick/lost, please take me to [destination]).

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT give the driver your 4-digit phone code until you are fully seated inside the car. The base fare for a DiDi in Chongqing is only ~10 RMB; just take a car if you are lost.

## Alternative
For hotpot: Order "Yuan Yang Guo" (鸳鸯锅, split pot) and say "Wei la" (微辣, mild). Do not ask for "mild" in English, they will still give you spicy.

- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Yuzhong District 8D terrain.
- **Applies To:** Chongqing city center.
