# Action Card: China Emergency SOS & Rescue

## Trigger
You are in immediate danger in China (lost passport, sudden illness, fire) and cannot speak Mandarin. 

## Immediate Action
Dial the direct emergency numbers. Do not wait for an English operator if it's life-threatening.
- Police: 110
- Ambulance: 120
- Fire: 119

## Show This / Action
For lost passports, you MUST get a police report to visit your embassy. Show this full-screen card to an officer:
"警察同志，我的护照丢了。我需要报警回执去大使馆补办。请帮帮我。"

For sudden illness, do not go to the outpatient hall. Show this to a taxi driver to go to the ER:
"师傅，我生病了。请马上带我去最近的国际医院或三甲医院急诊。谢谢！"

## Safety / Don't Do
Do not try to explain a complex emergency via a translation app. Show the pre-translated Chinese cards directly. Do not go to a normal clinic at night; go to a 3A hospital's Emergency Department (急诊科).

## Alternative
If you are completely lost, find a younger local (like a university student) and show them: "你好，我迷路了，能帮我指一下方向吗？谢谢！"

## Evidence

- **Tested On:** Guangzhou & Shanghai emergency protocols.
- **Applies To:** All foreign travelers in mainland China.
