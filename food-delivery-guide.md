# Action Card: Hotel Food Delivery Handoff

## Trigger
You order Meituan or Xiaoxiang delivery to your hotel. The rider calls you in Chinese, but your hotel doesn't allow riders upstairs. You don't know where your food is.

## Immediate Action
Do NOT answer the phone. Open the app's chat feature. Use a translation tool to send: "我是外国人，不懂中文。请放前台/外卖柜，不要打电话。谢谢。" (I am a foreigner, don't understand Chinese. Please leave at front desk/locker, do not call. Thanks.)

## Show This / Action
If the food goes to a smart locker, tap "取餐" (Pick up) on the screen. Enter the last 4 digits of your phone number. If it fails, scan the QR code on the screen with WeChat.

## Safety / Don't Do
Do NOT rely on the hotel robot during peak dining hours (6-8 PM). The robot is often busy or broken. You will need to go downstairs and match the bag's appearance using the rider's photo.

## Alternative
If your food is stolen from the front desk, go to the app order page, click "Request Refund" (申请售后) and select "Item not received" (未收到餐).

## Evidence
- **Last Verified:** October 2026
- **Tested On:** Guangzhou hotel front desks and smart lockers.
- **Applies To:** 3-star hotels and above.
- **Source:** [GoChinaTrip.app](https://gochinatrip.app/fresh-daily.html)
